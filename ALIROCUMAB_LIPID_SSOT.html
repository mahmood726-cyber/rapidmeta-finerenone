How to read this: Between-trial variation is high: I-squared is 87.9 per cent. The trials differ in background statin therapy, in population, and in dose regimen, so the pooled figure summarises a range of settings rather than estimating one common effect. A fixed-effect summary of the same data gives a narrower interval and would overstate what is known.
