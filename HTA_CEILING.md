## The unit, before any number

**OBJECT** = one file matching `ssot/*/*.json` carrying an `app_id`.
This is the unit of every denominator on this page. It is **not** a
page, **not** a rendered card, **not** an outcome, and **not** a topic
directory.

**OUTCOME** = one key under `results.by_outcome` of an OBJECT. An
object holds 0..n outcomes. **Outcomes are not topics** and the two
counts must never be quoted against each other's denominator.

**LIVE** = an outcome whose `pooled` block holds a point estimate and
is not withdrawn. Only a LIVE outcome can carry a Summary-of-Findings
or HTA row.

## Is the ceiling real, or the ceiling of what we stored?

Of the 128 objects holding no live pooled estimate:

| state | unit | n | meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `COMPUTABLE_NOT_COMPUTED` | OBJECT | 73 | two or more trials hold a 2x2 for the same outcome. A fixable gap; it raises the ceiling. |
| `SINGLE_TRIAL_CELLS_ONLY` | OBJECT | 2 | one trial holds cells. An estimate is possible, a pooled one is not. |
| `CELLS_TRIAL_LEVEL_ONLY` | OBJECT | 6 | arms or registration counts exist but describe the trial's PRIMARY endpoint, not a named outcome. A lead to chase by hand, never a derivation. |
| `NO_CELLS_AND_SIDECAR_WAS_READ` | OBJECT | 0 | looked at, nothing there. A real data gap. |
| `NOT_MEASURED_NO_SIDECAR_MAPPED` | OBJECT | 47 | **never looked at.** No `outputs/r_validation` sidecar resolved for the topic. NOT a data gap. |

Identity: 128 = 128.

| ceiling | unit | n |
|---|---|---|
| today | OBJECT | 27 of 155 |
| if the computable are computed | OBJECT | 100 of 155 |

**The second figure is a LOWER BOUND.** 47 of the 128 objects without an
estimate were never looked at for out-of-object cells, because the
topic-to-sidecar mapper resolved for only 74 of 128. Among the 81 that
**were** looked at, 73 were computable.

So the next lever is the **mapper**, not the evidence. Improving
topic-to-sidecar resolution is what moves this number, and until it
improves, `NOT_MEASURED_NO_SIDECAR_MAPPED` must never be quoted or
aggregated as though it meant `NO_CELLS`.

## Computable is not usable

Every one of the 73 objects classed `COMPUTABLE_NOT_COMPUTED` **already
holds a pooled estimate and interval** in its `outputs/r_validation`
sidecar. They are not merely computable; they are computed and unused.

But k is 2 or 3 for most of them, and a REML+HKSJ interval at k=2 with
high heterogeneity is enormous by construction. Quoting the raw count
as a ceiling would promise tables whose 95% interval spans seventy
orders of magnitude. Criterion, chosen on principle and not fitted:
an interval is DEGENERATE when `ci_high / ci_low > 1000`, or a bound is
non-positive or non-finite.

| state | unit | n |
|---|---|---|
| `USABLE` | OBJECT | 47 |
| `DIRECTION_CONFLICTED` | OBJECT | 1 |
| `DEGENERATE_INTERVAL` | OBJECT | 25 |

`DIRECTION_CONFLICTED` exists because interval width alone is not
enough, and that was demonstrated rather than theorised.
`pcsk9-inhibitors-cv-review` passed the width test at OR 6.35 (1.26 to
32.1) -- the wrong direction for PCSK9 inhibitors on cardiovascular
events. Its per-trial odds ratios run from 0.41 to 362: FOURIER and
ODYSSEY OUTCOMES sit near 0.84 (CV events, protective) while ORION-10,
ORION-11 and BERSON sit at 35 to 401, which is an LDL-C
target-attainment outcome where benefit means MORE events. The pool
combines quantities that are not the same quantity, and I-squared of
99.8% is the tell. A narrow interval around an incommensurable pool is
worse than a wide one, not better.

`USABLE` means the interval carries decision content and the
contributing trials do not contradict each other in direction. It does
**not** mean the estimate is correct or that the topic should be
published -- several carry I-squared above 90%, and that is a separate
judgement that this measurement does not make.

| ceiling | unit | n |
|---|---|---|
| today | OBJECT | 27 of 155 |
| plus computable, any interval | OBJECT | 100 of 155 |
| **plus computable with a usable interval** | OBJECT | **74 of 155** |

Identity across the 73 computable objects: 47 usable + 1 direction-conflicted + 25 degenerate = 73.

**The target of twenty is comfortable, not tight.**
