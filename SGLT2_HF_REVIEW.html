Q does not exceed its degrees of freedom, so the Higgins statistic sits at its floor and the between-study variance is estimated at zero. That is reported with the same caveat this batch applies everywhere rather than as reassurance: with four trials the statistic has little power to detect heterogeneity, and a floor value is consistent both with genuine agreement and with not being able to tell. What supports reading it as agreement here is visible without the statistic -- the four point estimates lie in a narrow band and every interval overlaps every other. The methods rule governing this decision

Decision: POOL four trials reporting the same effect measure for the same outcome CONCEPT against the same comparator, producing an AVERAGE ACROSS a documented difference in how two of them define the composite, across two molecules and across the ejection-fraction spectrum

Cochrane Handbook sections: 3.2.1, 3.2.3, 3.2.4, 10.9, 10.10.1, 10.10.2, 10.10.4, 10.14

Sections 3.2.1 and 3.2.3 fix the two axes on which ELIGIBILITY turns -- population and comparator -- and every screening decision names which of them it falls on, which is why the log has a criterion column rather than a verdict column. Section 3.2.4 is what keeps the MEASURE out of that list: it cautions that eligibility resting on which outcomes a study reported invites outcome reporting bias, so the measure is recorded in a separate column as a limit on POOLING. Section 10.9 is the authority for that limit, and it gives the hazard ratio as the effect measure for time-to-event data and sanctions combining log hazard ratios by generic inverse variance; all four trials report a Cox hazard ratio for the time to a first event of their primary composite against placebo, so the measure is shared and nothing is converted. What the pool crosses is documented under 10.10.1 in the uniformity table, and the first row of that table is the one that matters: two trials count an urgent heart failure visit inside the composite and two do not. That difference is not smoothed away, not harmonized and not described as absent. Section 10.10.4 governs the model and the between-study variance estimator; restricted maximum likelihood is used and the comparison of estimators that 10.10.4.4 motivates is RUN rather than asserted, and with this little heterogeneity the estimators agree closely, which is reported as the unremarkable result it is. Section 10.14 is why the leave-one-out analysis is here; unlike the other cardiology pool in this batch it changes nothing, and that is itself the finding.

Section 10.10.4.4 records that estimators other than DerSimonian and Laird perform better and that restricted maximum likelihood is now the default in the Cochrane software, and it is the whole of the authority for running this comparison. Nothing in the Handbook instructs a review to compare variance estimators at a particular number of studies; running it is a choice made under section 10.14. Here it is uninformative, and that is reported rather than dropped: a sensitivity analysis that changes nothing is evidence about the result, and only a lane that ran it can say so.

The three variance estimators agree, because there is no between-study variance for them to disagree about, and all three intervals exclude the null. The Hartung-Knapp-Sidik-Jonkman interval is wider, as its floor guarantees it can only be, and it excludes the null as well. No method tried here reverses the finding. This is reported as a null sensitivity result and not as corroboration: four ways of computing an interval from the same four numbers agreeing is arithmetic, not independent evidence.

Restricted maximum likelihood is kept as the headline estimator with the ordinary interval, because section 10.10.4.4 records it as the Cochrane default and as a better estimator than DerSimonian and Laird. With the between-study variance estimated at zero the choice is close to immaterial here, and keeping the default is therefore not a choice that flatters the result. The Hartung-Knapp-Sidik-Jonkman interval is reported beside it, with the floor on its scale factor applied so it can widen and can never come out narrower than the ordinary interval.

What the search found, and what was kept

The candidate set was assembled from the included-study LISTS of three published syntheses of this class in heart failure, taken together with the registry results records staged for every trial those lists name. That is the reconciliation step doing the work a search would do, and it is described here as what it is rather than as a systematic database search.

Eligible: ELIGIBILITY turns on population, intervention and comparator: a trial is in scope if it randomised adults with CHRONIC heart failure to an SGLT2 inhibitor against placebo on top of background therapy. It does NOT turn on which analysis the trial reported, because section 3.2.4 cautions that making eligibility depend on reported outcomes invites outcome reporting bias. What the measure governs is POOLING, under section 10.9: only a time-to-first-event hazard ratio can be combined with the others. Each row below therefore carries two separate columns -- the eligibility axis it fails, and, where it applies, the reason its quantity could not have been pooled even had it been eligible. Every screened trial appears with the quantity it reports quoted, whichever column it fell in.

Excluded, with reasons

  • SOLOIST-WHF -- fails population
    POPULATION, which is the axis the Handbook makes an eligibility criterion. It enrolled patients with type 2 diabetes who had been hospitalized for worsening heart failure and started treatment before or shortly after discharge, which is a recently-decompensated population rather than the chronic ambulatory one this question fixes. SEPARATELY, and recorded as a pooling limit rather than as a second exclusion: its primary endpoint is the TOTAL number of cardiovascular deaths, heart failure hospitalizations and urgent heart failure visits -- first AND subsequent events -- which its own abstract states in those words. That is a recurrent-event quantity, so under section 10.9 it could not have entered this pool even had the population matched. The two are kept apart because an earlier version of this object ran them together and cited a section that cautions against exactly that.

  • SCORED -- fails population
    POPULATION, and this is the one exclusion in this log that does NOT turn on the measure.   • EMPULSE -- fails population
    POPULATION and duration: patients were randomised in hospital during an admission for acute heart failure and treated for up to ninety days, which is neither the chronic population nor the event-driven follow-up this question fixes.     primary_oa: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35228754/

No fresh systematic database search was run for this object, and nothing here should be read as though one had been. The completeness claim rests on reconciliation against three independent published syntheses whose included-study lists are read rather than counted, plus the registry layer. A trial that all three syntheses missed and that no staged registry record names would be missed here too. That is a real and stated limit on the search, and it is the reason the completeness statement below is scoped to what was reconciled against rather than to the literature as a whole.

Reconciliation against the published synthesis of the same literature

Vaduganathan et al. SGLT-2 inhibitors in patients with heart failure: a comprehensive meta-analysis of five randomised controlled trials. Lancet 2022.

What could not be resolved

  • what a strictly harmonized pool of these same four trials would give
    This object pools each trial's OWN primary composite, and two of the four count an urgent heart failure visit that the other two do not. A pool of the strictly harmonized composite would be a different and arguably better quantity, and it cannot be computed here: the harmonized cell is published per trial for some of these trials and not for others, and constructing the missing ones would require patient-level data this object does not hold. What can be said is recorded rather than estimated -- the published syntheses that DID harmonize are held beside this pool with their values intact, and a reader can see how far apart the two approaches land. Attempting the harmonized pool from the values available would mean pooling published harmonized cells for two trials with own-primary cells for the others, which is the estimand mixing this object exists to refuse.
