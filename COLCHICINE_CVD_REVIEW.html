Estimate withdrawn. THE TRIALS DO NOT SHARE ONE ENDPOINT, AND ONE WHOLE COMPONENT IS PRESENT IN ONE AND ABSENT FROM THE OTHERS. COLCOT's registered primary, verbatim, is 'First Event of Cardiovascular Death, RESUSCITATED CARDIAC ARREST, Acute Myocardial Infarction, Stroke, or URGENT HOSPITALIZATION FOR ANGINA REQUIRING CORONARY REVASCULARIZATION' -- five components. LoDoCo2's, per this page's own benchmark record, is CV death, SPONTANEOUS myocardial infarction, ischaemic stroke, or ischaemia-driven coronary revascularisation. AND CONVINCE IS NOT A COMPOSITE TRIAL AT ALL. Its registration declares THREE SEPARATE PRIMARY OUTCOMES -- 'Recurrence of non-fatal ischemic stroke', 'non-fatal Major Cardiac event', and 'Vascular death' -- and the first of them counts RECURRENT NON-FATAL ISCHAEMIC STROKE AND NOTHING ELSE. A single-component stroke-recurrence outcome cannot be averaged with a five-component cardiovascular composite. SEPARATELY: THE PAGE CANNOT AGREE WITH ITSELF ABOUT HOW MANY TRIALS THIS IS. Its AUTO_INCLUDE_TRIAL_IDS names FIVE -- COLCOT, LoDoCo2, COPS, CLEAR SYNERGY and CONVINCE. Its embedded trial data carries THREE. The index card published k=2. Three different counts on one page, and nothing reconciles them. SEPARATELY AGAIN: THE PUBLISHED NUMBER IS NOT RECONSTRUCTABLE. The card served 'HR 0.75 (0.61 to 0.91), k=2'. Thirty-three candidate pools were computed from this page's own four published hazard ratios -- every subset of two, three and four trials, under fixed-effect, DerSimonian-Laird and REML -- and NONE gives 0.75 (0.61 to 0.91). The closest, LoDoCo2 with CONVINCE under DerSimonian-Laird, gives 0.755 (0.623 to 0.915). Search bounded at 33 pools and reported as bounded. AND WHICHEVER TWO TRIALS IT WAS, THE POOL EXCLUDES CLEAR SYNERGY IN SILENCE -- the largest trial here at 7,264 randomised, and the only one whose result is null at 0.99 (0.85 to 1.16). Nothing on the page states that it was left out.

WHAT IS CONFIRMED, STATED AS PROMINENTLY AS WHAT IS WRONG. (1) COLCOT'S EVENT COUNTS ARE THE REGISTRY'S OWN, EXACTLY: 131 of 2366 against 170 of 2379. (2) THE TRIAL IDENTITIES ARE RIGHT, and the page deserves credit for something this corpus has got wrong elsewhere: LoDoCo2 and COPS are Australian trials with no ClinicalTrials.gov registration, and the page carries them as 'ACTRN-LODOCO2' and 'ACTRN-COPS' RATHER THAN INVENTING NCT NUMBERS FOR THEM. On DOAC_CANCER_VTE the same situation produced a row keyed to an unrelated German trial. Here it did not. (3) THE DIRECTION OF THE UNDERLYING QUESTION IS NOT IN DOUBT and this withdrawal does not touch it: colchicine reduced events in COLCOT, 0.77 (0.61 to 0.96), and in LoDoCo2, 0.69 (0.57 to 0.83). What it did NOT do is reduce them in the two largest and most recent trials -- CLEAR SYNERGY 0.99 (0.85 to 1.16) and CONVINCE 0.84 (0.68 to 1.05) -- and a two-trial pool that drops the null ones is the specific thing that cannot stand here. It establishes that THIS one averaged endpoints that differ by whole components, over a trial count the page states three different ways, to reach a number none of its own data produces. THE REPLACEMENT QUESTION IS IDENTIFIED AND DELIBERATELY NOT TAKEN.
