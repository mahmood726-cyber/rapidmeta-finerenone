The served upper bound is out by 0.0762 -- seven and a half units in the last quoted place. Proved by a bound not by a search`scripts/pooled_point_in_achievable_range_gate.py`. Every inverse-variance pool, every Mantel-Haenszel and Peto pool and every fixed weighting is a convex combination of the per-trial log-effects, so a claimed point outside [min, max] cannot be produced by any estimator that has ever been written. Inside the hull the bound is the random-effects family itself: as tau-squared runs from 0 to infinity the weights run from the fixed-effect weights to equal weights, and the pooled point traces a path between the fixed-effect pool and the unweighted mean. COLCOT is the less precise of the two, so its weight can never exceed one half -- 40.7% at tau-squared 0, rising to 50% -- and reaching 0.745 would require it to carry seventy per cent. The achievable interval is 0.7215 to 0.7289. This is arithmetic, not an enumeration, and it covers estimators nobody has written yet.
The weaker result reported with its marginSeparately, no subset of any size of this page's four published hazard ratios -- COLCOT, CLEAR SYNERGY, CONVINCE, LoDoCo2 -- reproduces the served triple at any tau-squared under either a normal or a Knapp-Hartung interval. That result is thin and is not what closes this. Its nearest miss, LoDoCo2 with CONVINCE, is out by 0.0104 against a unit of 0.0100 in the last quoted place -- a margin of four per cent. It is recorded with that margin rather than stated as a verdict. It also cannot be the source: the benchmark names its two trials, and CONVINCE reported in 2024.
