Presented as a map until that is built, rather than as a false pooled number.

Method & honesty notes

  • Analysis type is declared per section and derived from the data (3 pairwise pools; 1 evidence map). None is dressed up as something it is not.
  • Counts come from the verified extraction ledger and each trial's identity/comparison was confirmed against ClinicalTrials.gov. Effects match the published landmark results (R21 ≈66%, CAB-LA large reduction, DTG superiority) — a strong external check. Next tier: upgrade each count to a PubMed-abstract / OA-table locator.
  • k=2–3: CIs are wide and uncertain; flagged, not trumpeted. HKSJ avoided (pathological at small k).
  • Built for low-resource settings — every value traceable to a public registry entry, no paywalled source.
