DomainRatingWhy it was rated that way
Risk of biasseriousThree separate features, each read from a staged source rather than assumed. PARACHUTE-HF describes itself as an 'open-label, multicenter randomized clinical trial' with blinded endpoint adjudication; blinded adjudication protects how an event is classified but not the unblinded management decisions that produce a heart failure hospitalization, which is one of the two components. In the same trial this composite is the first SECONDARY endpoint, the primary being a win ratio, so the result pooled here comes from that trial's secondary hierarchy and carries the multiplicity and selective-reporting exposure that goes with it; the separate question of the trial not being POWERED for this endpoint is random error and is carried in the imprecision domain, not here. PARADIGM-HF 'was stopped early, according to prespecified rules ... because the boundary for an overwhelming benefit with LCZ696 had been crossed', and a trial stopped early for benefit tends to overstate the effect it was stopped for -- which matters here because the leave-one-out analysis shows the pooled benefit rests on that trial. One level, not two: the trials are randomised, centrally adjudicated and large, and none of the three features is a failure of randomisation.

Projected manuscript draft

Every paragraph below is assembled from the canonical object at build time. Nothing here is written by the generator, and nothing recomputes when you open the page — this is the review saying what it holds, in the order a paper says it.

  1. Question. In adults with heart failure and reduced ejection fraction, what is the hazard of a first cardiovascular death or heart failure hospitalization with sacubitril/valsartan compared with enalapril?
  2. Outcome and estimand. Cardiovascular death or hospitalization for heart failure, whichever comes first, as a hazard ratio. Cox proportional hazards
  3. Search and selection. The search ran across PubMed and ClinicalTrials.gov for randomised comparisons of sacubitril/valsartan with enalapril in heart failure, and was then extended by the reconciliation step: the included-study list of every retrievable published synthesis of the same comparison was read and diffed against this one in both directions. That second pass is what found PARALLEL-HF, which the brief this object was built from did not name.
  4. Eligibility. A randomised trial of sacubitril/valsartan against enalapril, in adults with heart failure and reduced ejection fraction, reporting cardiovascular death or heart failure hospitalization as a time-to-first-event hazard ratio. All four conditions, and the last is a condition on the QUANTITY reported, not on the trial's quality: several excluded trials are large, well conducted and directly on topic, and report something that is not this.
  5. Synthesis method. random model, estimator REML, k = 3.
  6. Result. Pooled HR 0.8392 (0.7429 to 0.948, 95% interval). I-squared 14.9469%, tau-squared 0.0035.
  7. Robustness. The pooled benefit rests on PARADIGM-HF, and the table is blunter than that summary sounds. Every leave-one-out fit still points below the null, so the DIRECTION is stable. The INTERVAL is not: only the fit that drops PARALLEL-HF keeps the null outside it. Dropping PARADIGM-HF or dropping PARACHUTE-HF both leave an interval that includes the null, so two of the three fits do. That is the single most important qualification on the headline, it is what the imprecision domain of the GRADE assessment is counting, and it is stated here rather than left for a reader to derive from the table.
  8. Cross-engine check. The summary agrees to within a ten-millionth on the ratio scale for the point and both bounds, and to a few parts in ten million on the between-study variance. Q and its degrees of freedom match exactly. This is a statement about two ENGINES agreeing on one arithmetic, and it is not a claim that the trials agree with each other: what the pooled trials differ on is in the uniformity table, and a cross-engine check has nothing to say about that.
  9. Certainty. GRADE certainty: low. start high; risk_of_bias serious (-1), imprecision serious (-1); total -2 -> low
  10. What this review could not settle. The one apparent gap this search had is now closed, and closing it removed a trial rather than adding one: the patient total of the paywalled Chagas reviews exceeded the trials found here by a post hoc PARADIGM-HF subgroup, which is not a separate randomisation and is not recovered. The reconciliation block sets out the arithmetic and the deduplication reason. What is still unread is how those reviews themselves treated that subgroup, which their abstracts do not say.

The two engines report DIFFERENT I-squared values and neither is wrong. This object's is the Higgins statistic computed from Q and its degrees of freedom; metafor's is computed relative to the restricted-maximum-likelihood between-study variance. They are different quantities that share a name, a single engine could never reveal it, and the disagreement is recorded rather than resolved by adopting the other engine's number.
