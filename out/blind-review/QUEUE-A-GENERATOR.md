**44 classes. 386 findings. 135 of 141 topics touched. 35% of the deduplicated register.**

These are not forty-four unrelated edits. They are one family:

> **a page asserting a property it does not have.**

The same shape as the 744 unpopulated shells and the verdict-only questions — the
apparatus of a claim printed where the claim isn't. The fix in every case is the
house pattern: **derive, or refuse**. Where the property holds, say it; where it does
not, say that, or say nothing. Never print the assertion unconditionally.

Each row gives the page string, how many pages carry it, and what the page would
have to state instead.
