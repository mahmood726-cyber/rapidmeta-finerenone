## ⭐⭐ THE OVER-CREDIT, WHICH MATTERS AS MUCH AS ANY CRITICISM

**Google's DECISIVE verdict in our favour credits us with cautioning that *"real-world
effectiveness will be lower than trial efficacy due to adherence factors."* The page never says
it.** The grounding gate missed it because that gate checks QUOTED strings, and this attribution
carried no quotation marks.

⭐ **My own recall probe made the IDENTICAL error on the IDENTICAL claim** — both assembled an
absent claim out of words scattered across the page. Two instruments, one built here and one a
frontier model, failing the same way on the same sentence is worth more as evidence about the
failure mode than either instance alone.

### The attribution gate built to catch this class DOES NOT WORK, and is reported as such

It flagged 6 of 6 verdicts — a number that says nothing, because it has two demonstrated
false-positive mechanisms:

1. **the English article "a"** read as the document label A, so our own page's prose (*"A screen
   of eight records found…"*) was flagged as an attribution;
2. **the judge's own descriptive vocabulary** (`statistical rigor`, `sparse data`) counted as
   attributed content.

⛔ **It does not get to report a rate.** Tuning it further against these six verdicts would fit the
instrument to its own validation sample. **One over-credit is confirmed — by reading, not by the
gate — and that is the whole finding.**
