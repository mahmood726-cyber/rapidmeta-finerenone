An indirect comparison between two treatments never randomised against each other IS constructible here: they meet at a common comparator, which is what Handbook section 11.2.1 describes, and a star graph supplies that path even though it contains no closed loop. An earlier version of this object said the absence of a loop meant there was no path at all, which is wrong, and a reviewer was right to say so. What the absence of a loop actually costs is the CHECK: section 11.4.4 assesses disagreement between evidence sources, and 11.4.4.1 measures incoherence in a closed loop as the absolute difference between the direct and the indirect summary estimate. With no loop there is no such difference to compute, so the assumption that indirect and direct evidence agree cannot be tested at all here. NO INDIRECT ESTIMATE IS COMPUTED, and the reason is a judgement about TRANSITIVITY under 11.2.2 rather than a graph-theoretic impossibility: that section requires the sets of trials to be similar, on average, in all important factors other than the comparison being made, and these are not. Every edge meets the same comparator, so the graph is joined; what differs is WHEN the outcome was measured, one comparison ending its follow-up a fortnight later than the other two. One trial dosed at home rather than under observation, making it a measurement of effectiveness where the others measure efficacy. Those are the grounds, and they are differences in effect modifiers, which is what that section is about. That each comparison rests on one study is ALSO true and is NOT part of this ground: it is a limit on precision and on replication, not an effect modifier, and an earlier version wrongly folded it in. The missing loop is why the judgement cannot be checked rather than why it holds.

The schema carries a covariance term for a study contributing more than one contrast against a shared arm, because a network must. NO included study is multi-arm, so the term is not exercised here and no covariance is estimated. Recording that it is unexercised is the point: a facility that has never run is not evidence that it works.
