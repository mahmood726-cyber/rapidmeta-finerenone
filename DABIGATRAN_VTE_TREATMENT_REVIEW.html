Was a poolable estimand missed below the primary?

A refusal to pool that never looked below the primary is a withholding. Every trial's primary, secondary and other registered outcomes were read, and matching is on exact normalised title text — looser matching would compare endpoints by their names, so every count below is a lower bound.

  • OTHER_DOAC — NOT_ASSESSABLE: Fewer than two trials in this comparator family have posted results, so no shared endpoint can be pooled whatever the registrations declare.
  • VKA — 2 trial(s) with posted results share 8 endpoint(s) at some registered rank:
    • laboratory analyses (secondaries)
    • number of participants who died (any cause) (secondaries)
    • number of participants who died due to vte (secondaries)
    • number of participants with acute coronary syndrome (acs) (secondaries)
    • number of participants with recurrent symptomatic dvt (secondaries)
    • number of participants with recurrent symptomatic non-fatal pe (secondaries)
    • number of participants with recurrent symptomatic venous thromboembolism (vte) and deaths related to vte (primaries)
    • number of participants with recurrent symptomatic vte and all deaths (secondaries)
    A shared title at a shared comparator is a CANDIDATE, not a pool. The components still have to be read and no estimate is computed here.
