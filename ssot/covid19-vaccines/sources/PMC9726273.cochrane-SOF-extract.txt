and a reviewer reported its contents as ABSENT -- concluding that figures which
appear in it four times each had been fabricated. Two causes, both worth knowing:
the file is large, and the vaccine names are written with a NON-ASCII HYPHEN
(U+2010), so searching the ASCII spelling 'Gam-COVID' returns nothing while the
U+2010 spelling returns 38 hits. Normalise hyphens before concluding anything is
missing from the full file.
