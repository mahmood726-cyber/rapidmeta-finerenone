### (A) IS DECLINING TO POOL THE RIGHT CALL, OR A DODGE?
Declining to pool across the three vaccine trials is the **methodologically correct decision**, not a dodge.

**Rationale:**
Synthesizing Gam-COVID-Vac (Sputnik V, viral vector), CVnCoV (HERALD, mRNA), and BBIBP-CorV (inactivated virus) into a single summary risk ratio would violate fundamental meta-analytic principles of clinical and methodological homogeneity. These trials evaluated distinct vaccine platforms, operating with different case definitions, conducted across different geographic regions and time periods with differing baseline transmission rates and circulating SARS-CoV-2 variants. Combining them into one global "COVID-19 vaccine risk ratio" would generate a clinically meaningless composite effect size. The benchmark Cochrane review (Grana et al., 2022) similarly presents efficacy per vaccine candidate/platform rather than pooling across distinct technologies. Removing the pooled estimate rather than publishing an invalid summary statistic with a disclaimer is the proper epidemiological practice.
