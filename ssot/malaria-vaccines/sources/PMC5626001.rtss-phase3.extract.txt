VERBATIM EXTRACTS -- these are the sentences the object's cells were read from.

[DESIGN AND THE THREE GROUPS -- NOTE THE SHARED CONTROL]
"From March 27, 2009, until Jan 31, 2011, infants aged 6-12 weeks and children
aged 5-17 months were recruited and randomly assigned (1:1:1) by block
randomisation with minimisation by centre to one of three groups. One group
received RTS,S/AS01 at months 0, 1, and 2, followed by a booster dose at month 20
(R3R group); a second group received the RTS,S/AS01 primary vaccination series
with meningococcal serogroup C conjugate vaccine (Menjugate, Novartis, Basel,
Switzerland) instead of an RTS,S/AS01 booster (R3C group); and the third group
received only comparator vaccines: rabies vaccine (Verorab, Sanofi Pasteur,
Paris, France) for children and Menjugate for young infants (C3C [control
group])."

The R3R and R3C groups are BOTH compared against the SAME C3C control group.
Both contrasts use the same control participants and the same control episode
count. Using both in one pooled estimate would count those controls twice.

[ESTIMAND -- THIS IS A RECURRENT-EVENT RATE ANALYSIS, NOT A BINARY ONE]
"Efficacy against all episodes of malaria was analysed by negative binomial
regression with follow-up time as offset, allowing for interdependence between
episodes within the same participant. Overall estimates were adjusted for study
site as a fixed effect"

"Vaccine efficacy (VE) against clinical malaria was analysed by negative binomial
regression and against severe malaria by relative risk reduction."

The unit of analysis for clinical malaria is the EPISODE, and the denominator is
person-time. The counts below are episode counts, not numbers of participants,
and they cannot be placed in a two-by-two table of participants.

[CASE DEFINITION]
"The primary case definition for clinical malaria was an illness accompanied by an
axillary temperature of at least 37.5 degrees C and asexual parasitaemia (>5000
parasites per mm3)"

[FOLLOW-UP WINDOW -- THIS IS THE FINAL 3-4 YEAR ANALYSIS, NOT THE 12-MONTH ONE]
"we initially designed this trial to assess vaccine efficacy (VE), safety, and
immunogenicity during 32 months of follow-up, but the protocol was amended
before month 32, on Dec 1, 2010, to extend the follow-up period to Dec 31, 2013
(median follow-up 48 months for children and 38 months for young infants)."

"Children were followed up for a median of 48 months (IQR 39-50) and young
infants for 38 months (34-41) after dose 1."

[CHILDREN 5-17 MONTHS -- THE PRIMARY RTS,S CELL IN THIS OBJECT]
"In the modified ITT population, from month 0 until study end, compared with 9585
episodes of clinical malaria that met the primary case definition in children in
the C3C group, 6616 episodes occurred in children in the R3R group (VE 36.3%,
95% CI 31.8-40.5) and 7396 in the R3C group (28.3%, 23.3-32.9)"

[YOUNG INFANTS 6-12 WEEKS -- A SEPARATE RANDOMISED COHORT]
"In the modified ITT population, from month 0 until study end, compared with 6170
episodes of clinical malaria that met the primary case definition in young
infants in the C3C group, 4993 episodes occurred in the R3R group (VE 25.9%, 95%
CI 19.9-31.5) and 5444 in the R3C group (18.3%, 11.7-24.4)"

[THE 12-MONTH CO-PRIMARY ENDPOINTS -- A DIFFERENT WINDOW, AND NOTE THE CI LEVEL]
"The coprimary endpoints of efficacy to clinical malaria over the first 12 months
after dose 3 were 55.8% (97.5% CI 50.6-60.4) in children aged 5-17 months and
31.3% (23.6-38.3) in infants aged 6-12 weeks."

These intervals are 97.5 per cent intervals, not 95 per cent intervals. This
paper does not state which estimand produced them, and reports them only as a
back-reference to earlier publications of the same cohort. This object therefore
carries them as a labelled row and does not pool them.

[WHAT MODEL PRODUCED THAT 12-MONTH FIGURE -- READ BOTH FIELDS, NOT ONE]
The registered record NCT00866619 posts this endpoint with two facts that are
easy to mistake for one, and staging only the first of them caused a real error
in review. VERBATIM from the outcome measure:

  title:          "Rate of First or Only Clinical Episode of Plasmodium
                   Falciparum (P. Falciparum) Malaria Infection (CPFMI), or
                   Clinical Malaria Episode of Primary Case Definition
                   (CPFMI-PCD)"
  unitOfMeasure:  "events per person-year"
  description:    "The time to first or only CPFMI-PCD is expressed in terms of
                   rate of first or only CPFMI (RfoCPFMI), that is person-year
                   rate in each group (n/T)."

and VERBATIM from the ANALYSIS attached to that same outcome:

  statisticalMethod: "Regression, Cox"
  paramType:         "Vaccine efficacy"   paramValue: 55.8
  ciPctValue:        97.5   ciLowerLimit: 50.6   ciUpperLimit: 60.4
  groupDescription:  "The analysis aimed to compare RfoCPFMI between groups over
                      the Months 2.5-14 time period. Using RfoCFPMI, a Cox
                      regression model was used to evaluate vaccine efficacy
                      (VE) allowing for adjustment by factors. VE was calculated
                      as 1 minus [Hazard Ratio (HR) in GSK257049 [5-17M] Group
                      (HR1) divided by HR in control VeroRab Comparator [5-17M]
                      Group (HR2)]; i.e. 1 - (HR1/HR2)."

So the ENDPOINT is expressed as a person-year rate, and the EFFECT ESTIMATE of
55.8 per cent is one minus a HAZARD RATIO from a Cox model. The two are not in
conflict; they describe different things. An earlier version of this extract
quoted only the unit and the description, and on that partial evidence a
reviewer concluded the effect was a rate ratio and this object relabelled it as
one. A later review read the analysis field and corrected it back. Both fields
are staged here so the question cannot be decided again on half the record.

[NOTE ON THE NUMBER 5949]
No figure of 5949 appears anywhere in this paper as a denominator for an
R3R-only contrast. The modified-ITT children total is 8922 across all three
groups. Any denominator that combines the R3R and R3C groups describes the
pooled RTS,S-vaccinated arm and cannot be used for a contrast that names R3R
alone.
