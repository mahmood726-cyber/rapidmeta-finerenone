[NOTE ON TWO DIFFERENT FIGURES BOTH PRINTED AS "72 PER CENT"]
This paper reports an all-sites 72 per cent twice, from two different analyses:
the multiple-episode analysis under the primary case definition gives an interval
of 69 to 75, and the time-to-first analysis under the SECONDARY case definition
gives an interval of 68 to 75. They are not the same quantity and their intervals
differ. Neither is a proportion of participants and neither may be converted to a
two-by-two table.

[NOTE ON AN INTERNAL DISCREPANCY IN THIS PAPER]
The Discussion states: "the primary analysis shows vaccine efficacy against
clinical malaria of 75% (72-79) at seasonal sites and 67% (59-73) at standard
sites". Both the Abstract and the Results state the co-primary time-to-first
figures as 75% (71-79) seasonal and 68% (61-74) standard; 67% (59-73) is the
standard-site MULTIPLE-EPISODE figure. The Discussion has mixed the two
analyses. This object uses the Abstract and Results figures.
