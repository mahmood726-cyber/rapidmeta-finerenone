EXTRACTS FROM THE ABSTRACT AND REPOSITORY RECORD:

Screening: "After duplicate removal, 832 records were screened and 60 were
selected for full-text screening, after which 8 were included in a systematic
review and meta-analysis"

Search period: studies published between 2000 and 2024.
Databases: PubMed, Scopus, Cochrane.

Age groups analysed: 6-12 weeks, and 5-17 months.

Reported pooled results:
  "RTS,S/AS01 malaria vaccine provides significant protection in the 6-12-week
  age group, with a 23.0% malaria risk reduction and risk ratio (RR) of 0.77."

  For the 5-17 month group: "the RR for malaria risk reduction was 0.61 for both
  vaccines."

  R21: "high efficacy with a low event rate of 0.22" alongside increased adverse
  events.

Other stated findings: protective efficacy declined over time, emphasising
booster necessity; RTS,S/AS01 demonstrated robust safety and antibody response.

WHAT THIS OBJECT TAKES FROM IT: the eligible-trial list, as a search prior and a
completeness check. This object located its trials independently and then
checked them against this review's stated count.

WHAT THIS OBJECT DOES NOT TAKE FROM IT: any effect estimate. The reasons are
recorded in the object's reconciliation block and rest on the wording above.
