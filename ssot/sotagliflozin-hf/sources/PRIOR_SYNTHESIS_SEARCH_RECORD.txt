PRIOR-SYNTHESIS SEARCH RECORD -- sotagliflozin (SOLOIST-WHF and SCORED)
Run 2026-08-10 against PubMed E-utilities. CORRECTED the same day, after a gate
leg found a paper this record had said did not exist.

WHY THIS FILE EXISTS. Every cascade cell records the prior-synthesis layer as
not available for the trials own primary endpoint. An absence claim with nothing
behind it is an assumption, so this is what was asked and what came back.

WHAT THE FIRST VERSION OF THIS FILE GOT WRONG. It concluded that no synthesis
reporting either trials own primary could be located. That was too strong. A
cross-family gate leg found, in one step:

  PMID 39257196 -- Bantounou et al, ESC Heart Fail 2025;12(2):968-979.
    Meta-analysis of sotagliflozin, a dual sodium-glucose-cotransporter 1/2
    inhibitor, for heart failure in type 2 diabetes.
    Nine studies, n = 15,320. Searched inception to 2 June 2023.
    Reports RELATIVE RISKS: heart failure RR 0.66 (0.64, 0.69) from 8 studies;
    MACE RR 0.73 (0.66, 0.81) from 8 studies; and a systolic blood pressure
    mean difference. It also reports numbers needed to treat.

It is now STAGED as OA_PM_BANTOUNOU2025_abstract.txt and registered as a source.

WHAT IT DOES AND DOES NOT SETTLE. It does not yield any cell in this object, and
the reason is the QUANTITY, not availability: it pools relative risks across nine
studies of mixed design, and restates neither trials own primary -- a
total-occurrence composite reported as a hazard ratio -- as a separate row. Its
abstract does not name its included studies, so its study list cannot be read
from what is open. Its search also closed before either trials FDA review was
published, so it cannot speak to the time-to-first analyses at all. The layer is
therefore unavailable FOR THIS CELL, which is a different and weaker statement
than the one this file made first.

TERMS RUN (esearch, db=pubmed), all on 2026-08-10:
  1. sotagliflozin AND (SOLOIST OR SCORED) AND (meta-analysis[pt] OR pooled)
  2. sotagliflozin pooled analysis SOLOIST-WHF SCORED heart failure
  3. Szarek sotagliflozin SOLOIST SCORED
  4. sotagliflozin SOLOIST-WHF SCORED combined analysis cardiovascular

RESULT. Terms 2 and 4 returned nothing. Terms 1 and 3 returned class-level and
unrelated work. Two class-level network meta-analyses were fetched and read --
PMID 40324721 (Su et al, Diabetes Res Clin Pract 2025) and PMID 38845687 (Lan et
al, Front Cardiovasc Med 2024) -- and neither names SOLOIST or SCORED in its
abstract.

THE LESSON, recorded because it is the point of keeping this file. Four queries
that all miss a paper are four queries, not a proof of absence. The queries were
built around the TRIAL ACRONYMS; the paper that exists is indexed under the DRUG
and the disease and names neither acronym in its title or abstract. An absence
claim is only ever as strong as the search that produced it, and this one was
weaker than the sentence it was used to write.

WHAT IS RECONCILED AGAINST. Vaduganathan et al 2022 (Lancet), staged here, is a
five-trial synthesis that DOES name SOLOIST-WHF in its abstract, so the
included-study-list check is possible against it. It reports a pooled value on a
harmonized first-event composite and no per-trial row, so it is a scope check and
not a value check, and it is recorded as exactly that.
