AMBITION-cm -- Single-Dose Liposomal Amphotericin B Treatment for Cryptococcal Meningitis
PMID 35320642 | PMCID PMC7612678 | DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2111904
N Engl J Med, 24 March 2022. Registration ISRCTN72509687.
Retrieved from PubMed Central full text (open access author manuscript).

DESIGN
  Open-label phase-III randomised controlled NON-INFERIORITY trial, 1:1,
  eight hospitals in Botswana, Malawi, South Africa, Uganda, Zimbabwe.
  HIV-positive adults, first episode of cryptococcal meningitis.

ARMS (exactly two)
  AmBisome arm : single dose liposomal amphotericin B 10 mg/kg
                 + flucytosine 100 mg/kg/day x 14 days
                 + fluconazole 1200 mg/day x 14 days      (three-drug regimen)
  Control arm  : amphotericin B deoxycholate 1 mg/kg/day + flucytosine
                 100 mg/kg/day for 7 days, then fluconazole 1200 mg/day
                 days 8-14                                 (two-drug regimen)

PRIMARY ENDPOINT
  All-cause mortality within 10 weeks after randomisation.
  Powered to show non-inferiority at a 10% ABSOLUTE margin
  (n=390/arm gives 90% power for the upper limit of the ONE-SIDED 95% CI
  of the absolute difference in percentage risks to be within 10%).

PRIMARY ANALYSIS POPULATION
  Intention-to-treat = all randomised who did not meet a late exclusion
  criterion. 844 randomised; 30 excluded (24 late-exclusion, 5 no
  cryptococcal meningitis, 1 HIV negative) -> 814 in ITT.
  NONE lost to follow-up.

PRIMARY RESULT (intention-to-treat, 10-week all-cause mortality)
  AmBisome : 101 / 407 died = 24.8%   (95% CI 20.7 to 29.3)
  Control  : 117 / 407 died = 28.7%   (95% CI 24.4 to 33.4)

  PRIMARY ESTIMAND, AS THE TRIAL DEFINED AND REPORTED IT:
    absolute difference in 10-week mortality RISK = -3.9 percentage points
    upper limit of the ONE-SIDED 95% CI = 1.2 percentage points
    within the pre-specified 10 percentage point non-inferiority margin
    p for non-inferiority < 0.001

  The trial's stated analysis method: "A generalized linear model with a
  binomial distribution was used to calculate mortality differences."
  The effect measure is a RISK DIFFERENCE, not a ratio and not a hazard.

PRE-SPECIFIED SUPERIORITY ANALYSIS AT 10 WEEKS (secondary to the above)
  unadjusted : risk difference -3.9 pp, TWO-SIDED 95% CI -10.0 to 2.2
               (not statistically significant)
  adjusted   : risk difference -5.7 pp, TWO-SIDED 95% CI -11.4 to -0.04
               (adjusting for site, age, sex, baseline Glasgow Coma Scale,
               CD4 count, CSF cryptococcal CFU/mL, ART status, haemoglobin,
               CSF opening pressure)

TIME-TO-EVENT
  Verbatim from the Results section:
    "Outcomes of time-to-event analyses of mortality risk using Cox
     regression are shown in [table], with no significant differences
     between groups."
  NO hazard ratio value appears anywhere in the full text retrieved from
  PMC. The Cox analysis is a supplementary table, is not the primary
  endpoint, and the paper reports no point estimate for it in the text.
  ==> Any hazard ratio attributed to AMBITION-cm (e.g. 0.86) is NOT the
      trial's primary estimand and is not verifiable from the primary
      publication. Do not store it as the trial's effect.

PER-PROTOCOL SENSITIVITY (30 further exclusions)
  AmBisome :  95 / 388 = 24.5%  (95% CI 20.3 to 29.1)
  Control  : 113 / 396 = 28.5%  (95% CI 24.1 to 33.3)
  risk difference -4.1 pp, upper one-sided 95% CI 1.1 pp

OTHER OUTCOMES
  CSF fungal clearance rate  : -0.40 logCFU/mL/day AmBisome
                               -0.42 logCFU/mL/day control
                               difference in mean rates 0.017
                               (95% CI -0.001 to 0.036)
  Paradoxical IRIS           : 15 / 407 (3.7%) AmBisome
                               19 / 407 (4.7%) control
  Culture-positive relapse   : 0 AmBisome; 1 control
  Re-admission (10 weeks)    : 71 / 407 (17.4%) both arms
  Mortality at 2, 4 and 16 weeks: AmBisome non-inferior at the 10% margin
                               at every time point, ITT and per-protocol.
                               (numeric values are in a table not carried
                               in the PMC text body -- NOT staged, so no
                               object cell may claim them)

SAFETY (safety population = all randomised receiving >=1 dose)
  grade 3 or 4 AE : 210 / 420 (50.0%) AmBisome vs 263 / 422 (62.3%) control, p<0.001
  grade 4 AE      :  91 / 420 (21.7%) vs 127 / 422 (30.1%), p=0.005
  grade 3/4 anaemia: 56 / 420 (13.3%) vs 165 / 422 (39.1%), p<0.001
  transfusion     :  32 / 420 (7.6%) vs 76 / 422 (18.0%)
  grade 3/4 creatinine rise: 22 / 420 (5.2%) vs 25 / 422 (5.9%)
  thrombophlebitis:   8 / 420 (1.9%) vs 28 / 422 (6.7%), p=0.001

NOTE ON DENOMINATORS
  The ITT denominator is 407 per arm. The SAFETY denominator is 420 and 422.
  These are different populations and must never be mixed in one row.
