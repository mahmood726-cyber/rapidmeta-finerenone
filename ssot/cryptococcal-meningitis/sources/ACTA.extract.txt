DESIGN (partially factorial, five treatment arms)
  HIV-infected adults with cryptococcal meningitis randomised to one of
  three INDUCTION STRATEGIES:
    (1) oral regimen: fluconazole 1200 mg/day + flucytosine 100 mg/kg/day,
        2 weeks
    (2) 1 week of amphotericin B 1 mg/kg/day
    (3) 2 weeks of amphotericin B 1 mg/kg/day
  Each patient assigned to amphotericin B was ADDITIONALLY randomised to
  receive fluconazole OR flucytosine as the partner drug.
  ==> Strategies (2) and (3) each split into two arms, giving FIVE arms:
        1wk-AmB + flucytosine, 1wk-AmB + fluconazole,
        2wk-AmB + flucytosine, 2wk-AmB + fluconazole,
        oral fluconazole + flucytosine.
  All patients then received fluconazole consolidation and were followed
  to 10 weeks.

RANDOMISED
  721 patients underwent randomisation.
  The three analysed strategy groups total 225 + 224 + 229 = 678.
  The abstract does not state the disposition of the remaining 43.

MORTALITY BY STRATEGY -- the only arm-level counts the abstract gives
                   2 weeks               10 weeks
  oral regimen     18.2%  (41 / 225)     35.1%  (79 / 225)
  1 wk amphB       21.9%  (49 / 224)     36.2%  (81 / 224)
  2 wk amphB       21.4%  (49 / 229)     39.7%  (91 / 229)
  arithmetic check, computed here from the published counts:
    41/225=18.22%  49/224=21.88%  49/229=21.40%
    79/225=35.11%  81/224=36.16%  91/229=39.74%   -- all consistent

NON-INFERIORITY RESULT (2-week mortality, ONE-SIDED 97.5% CI on the
                        difference in percentage points)
  oral regimen vs 2-week amphotericin B : upper limit 4.2 pp
  1-week amphotericin B vs 2-week amphB : upper limit 8.1 pp
  Both below the predefined 10-percentage-point non-inferiority margin.
  NOTE THE ESTIMAND: an absolute RISK DIFFERENCE with a one-sided 97.5%
  bound, on 2-WEEK mortality -- not 10-week, and not a ratio.

PARTNER-DRUG COMPARISON (pooled across BOTH amphotericin strategies)
  flucytosine partner :  71 deaths = 31.1%
  fluconazole partner : 101 deaths = 45.0%
  hazard ratio for death at 10 weeks 0.62, 95% CI 0.45 to 0.84, P = 0.002
  arithmetic check, computed here:
    implied n on flucytosine  = 71/0.311  = 228.3
    implied n on fluconazole  = 101/0.450 = 224.4   -> total 452.7
    amphotericin patients      = 224 + 229 = 453     -- consistent
    deaths in amphotericin arms at 10 weeks = 81 + 91 = 172
    deaths in the partner comparison        = 71 + 101 = 172 -- identical
  ==> The partner comparison is a WITHIN-amphotericin contrast over the
      same 453 patients and the same 172 deaths already counted in the
      strategy rows. It is NOT an independent body of evidence and must
      not be added to a synthesis that already contains the strategy rows.

BEST SINGLE ARM
  1 week of amphotericin B PLUS FLUCYTOSINE had the lowest 10-week
  mortality: 24.2%, 95% CI 16.2 to 32.1.
  This is a PERCENTAGE with a confidence interval. The abstract gives NO
  numerator and NO denominator for this arm.

WHAT THE ABSTRACT DOES NOT PROVIDE  -- hard limits on this build
  * NO arm-level events/total for any of the five arms. The 24.2% figure
    above is the only arm-level number, and it is a percentage only.
  * NO arm-level counts for the four other arms at any time point.
  * NO variance, covariance or correlation structure for the multi-arm
    trial.
  * NO 10-week comparison of the oral regimen against either amphotericin
    strategy as a ratio measure.
  ==> A five-node network in which each ACTA arm is its own node CANNOT
      be built from an accessible source. Constructing arm-level 2x2
      tables by halving the strategy denominators would be FABRICATION:
      the partner randomisation is not guaranteed 1:1 within strategy,
      and the mortality split between partners is known only in POOLED
      form (71 vs 101 across both strategies together).
      The recoverable network is at STRATEGY level (three ACTA nodes),
      with the partner drug recorded as a documented within-node
      difference and its own separate within-trial contrast.

SAFETY
  Verbatim: "Side effects, such as severe anemia, were more frequent with
  2 weeks than with 1 week of amphotericin B or with the oral regimen."
  No counts are given in the abstract.

CONCLUSION (verbatim)
  "One week of amphotericin B plus flucytosine and 2 weeks of fluconazole
  plus flucytosine were effective as induction therapy for cryptococcal
  meningitis in resource-limited settings."

RELATION TO AMBITION-cm
  AMBITION-cm's control arm is 7 days of amphotericin B deoxycholate
  1 mg/kg/day + flucytosine, then fluconazole 1200 mg/day days 8-14.
  That is the SAME regimen as ACTA's "1 week amphotericin B + flucytosine"
  arm -- the arm ACTA identified as best, and the regimen WHO adopted in
  2018 following ACTA. AMBITION-cm's own discussion states its results are
  "consistent with the relatively favorable outcomes with the 1-week
  amphotericin B deoxycholate plus flucytosine regimen that were reported
  in the ACTA trial."
  This shared regimen is the ONLY candidate common comparator joining the
  two trials -- and on the ACTA side it is precisely the arm for which no
  events/total is published.
