================================================================================
T1 -- CANONICAL TRIALS ARE A HARD-CODED ALLOW-LIST. CONFIRMED.
================================================================================

    AUTO_INCLUDE_TRIAL_IDS=new Set(["NCT01453608","NCT02937454","NCT02642562",
    "NCT03037931"])

    isValidScreenIncludeCandidate=(trial,referenceData=null)=>{
      const id=String(trial?.id??"").toUpperCase();
      return !!AUTO_INCLUDE_TRIAL_IDS.has(id)
        ||(!(!referenceData||!referenceData[id]
             ||isPhaseTwoLike(referenceData[id]?.phase))
           ||isFutureCriteriaMatchedRCT(trial))}

Membership of the allow-list is by itself sufficient for inclusion, and a trial
that is NOT on it must instead survive a reference-data lookup or the
future-RCT classifier. That is the "canonical means the only trials allowed in"
architecture the batch note calls GATING.

THE ALLOW-LIST NAMES FOUR TRIALS AND FAIR-HF2 IS NOT ONE OF THEM. The set holds
CONFIRM-HF, AFFIRM-AHF, IRONMAN and HEART-FID. NCT03036462 is absent. So the
largest and most recent ferric carboxymaltose outcome trial had to pass the
future-RCT classifier to be seen at all -- and the classifier is keyed on drug
names, while FAIR-HF2's registry record names its intervention only as "Iron".
The two defects compound: the trial is neither pre-approved nor discoverable.

================================================================================
T2 -- RoB "some-concerns" IS SILENTLY COERCED TO "low". CONFIRMED, and the
       coercion is broader than the claim.
================================================================================

    safeRob=rob=>{const valid=["low","some","high"];
      return Array.isArray(rob)?rob.map(r=>valid.includes(r)?r:"low")
                               :["low","low","low","low","low"]}

The accepted vocabulary is `low`, `some`, `high`. The RoB 2 term written in the
data is `some-concerns`, which is NOT in that list, so `.map` sends it to
**"low"**. Every unrecognised domain value becomes low risk, and a `rob` field
that is not an array at all becomes five low-risk domains.

The downstream consumer then computes the overall judgement from the coerced
vector:

    overallRob = rob.includes("high") ? "high"
               : rob.includes("some") ? "some" : "low"

so a trial whose real assessment was "some concerns" on every domain is
presented as low risk of bias throughout, and any GRADE derivation reading that
vector inherits the inflation. A silent default that always fails SAFE-LOOKING
rather than loud is the shape of this defect: it can only ever raise certainty.

================================================================================
T4 -- THE FUTURE-RCT CLASSIFIER IS THE ONLY ROUTE IN FOR A NON-CANONICAL TRIAL
================================================================================

    isFutureCriteriaMatchedRCT(trial)

appears in the page and is, per the include predicate above, the sole path by
which a search hit outside the allow-list can be admitted. That places the whole
completeness claim on one classifier, which is why the rebuild's completeness
statement rests on a REDISCOVERY TEST -- run against ClinicalTrials.gov, with
its queries and results staged -- rather than on an assertion that a search ran.
