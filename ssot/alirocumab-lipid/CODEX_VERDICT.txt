The heterogeneity also reconstructs: tau-squared 47.4240, I-squared 87.9246 percent, Q 41.4066 on 5 df. Those round to "tau2": 47.42, "i2": 87.9, "q": 41.41, and "df": 5.

The secondary-outcome inclusion is adequately disclosed and not intrinsically biased for this estimand. The object says "This trial's only PRIMARY outcome is the percentage of participants experiencing adverse events" and the source confirms the LDL-C result is SECONDARY. Since the synthesized endpoint is the same LDL-C week-24 ITT effect, including it is appropriate if the review question is outcome-based rather than primary-outcome-only.

The "superseded" block is honest as an audit trail because it names the five trials. Recomputing the named k=5 set gives -52.5809 with 95 percent CI -60.6057 to -44.5561, matching "point": -52.58, "ci_low": -60.61, and "ci_high": -44.56. It would only mislead if rendered as a second concurrent analysis rather than historical provenance.

The opposite arm order is handled correctly. NCT01709500 lists alirocumab first in the source, and the analysis is alirocumab versus placebo with a negative LS mean difference, so the treatment-favouring direction is right.
