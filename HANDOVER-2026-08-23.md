The test is one question: *if this work succeeds completely, does the control still hold?* If no, key it to a fixture, a pinned revision, or a property of the instrument. `lint_field_name_in_reader_prose` was pinned to `a2091846a` for exactly this reason and survives; this one was not and does not.

## 4. Corrections to my own claims

**I reported the re-roll as "finished long ago."** It was at **106 of 157 and still running**. Had that gone unchallenged, the served-bytes verification would have been run against a partial rebuild and reported as complete.

**I introduced a content-loss defect while fixing one.** The zero-drop work replaced `if r.get("point")` with `is not None` across 13 sites — correct, because a pooled point of exactly `0.0` is the null for a mean or risk difference and was rendering as *"not pooled"*. But on a **log-scale forest plot**, admitting `0.0` means `log(0)`, which is undefined, and the figure returned empty. `LENACAPAVIR_PREP_SSOT` lost **two forest plots** and shrank 7.32%.

> **A defect and a protection can be the same line of code.** The truthiness test was excluding zero *accidentally*, and that accident was the only thing keeping `log(0)` out of the transform. Removing the defect removed the protection.

Fixed by testing **scale** rather than truthiness — non-positive points are unplottable on a ratio scale and skipped there, and drawn normally on a linear one. That is a correct fix, not a revert: the zero-drop remains fixed everywhere it was a defect. **Forest plots 16 → 16, page +0.10%.**

**Earlier in the day I also withdrew:** an over-broad fabrication claim (three topics called invented; only one is), a donor-identifier count (727, then 149, then **no number** — the matcher compared filename substrings against registry text), a bare-count refutation that was itself wrong, and a "stale build" and "deploy gap" that were text-mode reads of CRLF.
