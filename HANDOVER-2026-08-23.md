## 2. The withdrawals — five numbers, unsoftened

Every one was caught before it reached a decision, and **every one was caught by measuring
rather than by reasoning harder**. A handover listing only the surviving numbers would teach
the wrong lesson.

| withdrawn | was | actually | why it was wrong |
|---|---|---|---|
| donor identifiers, pass 1 | **727 of 745 (97.6%)** | — | every legacy page hardcodes `trialData=["NCT01035255","NCT01920711","NCT02924727"]` (LCZ696/ARNI trials) inside its engine. Read out of `<script>`, template code looked like citations to another subject. |
| donor identifiers, pass 2 | **149 of 602** | **no number** | the matcher compared filename tokens against registry text. `ALS_NEW_AGENTS` → terms `['agents']` vs *"Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis"*; `ALK_NSCLC` vs *"Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer"*. Correct trials, scored as donors. |
| bare-count refutation | "128 of 129 carry both, so the distinction fails" | **1 of 129 — the distinction holds** | extraction counted any NCT anywhere on the page. Zero were among the numbers flagged unverified. The banner already said *"no resolvable trial id"*. |
| SGLT2 "stale build" | 13 days stale | not stale | the label read the **object's** `built` date, not the page's generation time. |
| local/origin "deploy gap" | 752-byte divergence | byte-identical | text-mode read converted CRLF→LF while `git show` returned raw bytes. 752 was the line count. |

**The donor limb is NOT VALIDATED and must not be given a number.** Detecting a donor needs
concept matching — acronym expansion, synonyms, MeSH — not substring overlap. The two
confirmed donors were confirmed **by reading**: `TIRZEPATIDE_ARDS` cites andexanet alfa
trials, `ICAGEN` cites edoxaban trials.

**The asymmetry is the general rule:** the *absence* limb needs no judgement — it is the
registry's own 404, re-queryable — so `57 pages cite identifiers that do not exist` is
publishable. The *donor* limb needs judgement, so it must abstain. **A check that requires
judgement should be built to abstain, not to guess.**

## 3. The lessons are in the source, not in a report

They will still be there when this conversation is gone.

| where | lesson |
|---|---|
| `ssot/paper_projector.py::_flatten_container` | **A check built to find what the fix removed can only agree with its author.** The container-repr class was declared closed by a probe searching for braces — which is exactly what the fix deletes. It left the field names: `bar:` still sits in delivered prose. Also: **a probe sees only the shape it was written for, and a shape distinction reads as a disagreement between people** (two lanes on ARNI, both correct, different shapes). |
| `scripts/instrument_controls.py::same_bytes` | **Read bytes, compare bytes.** Refuses a `str` argument outright. |
| `…::plant_and_require` | **Plant the defect, require the gate to find it.** A gate never seen to fail is a log line. |
| `…::authored_not_constructed` | **A channel nothing validates is where silent corruption survives.** A `-m` commit message had `` `_REVIEW` `` command-substituted out of it and every check passed. |
| `…::every_referring_surface` | **A change is applied to the surfaces its author was thinking about, and not to the one they were not.** Three instances today. |
| `…::page_states_its_own_condition` | **When a page states its own condition and your instrument disagrees, the page is the evidence and the instrument is the hypothesis.** |
| `…::abstain_or_answer` | Returns `NOT_ASSESSABLE` rather than guessing. |
