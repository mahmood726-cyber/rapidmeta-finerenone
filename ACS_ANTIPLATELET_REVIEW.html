Estimate withdrawn. The four trials do not answer one question, and one of them does not measure an ischaemic event at all. This estimate averaged four different comparisons into a single pairwise odds ratio. (1) the contrasts differ: plato is ticagrelor against clopidogrel; TRITON-TIMI 38 is prasugrel against clopidogrel; ISAR-REACT 5 is ticagrelor against prasugrel; Twilight is ticagrelor plus aspirin against ticagrelor alone, which is an aspirin-withdrawal question and not a P2Y12 comparison. Four contrasts over three drugs plus an aspirin question is a network, and a network cannot be reduced to one pairwise pool. (2) twilight contributes a bleeding endpoint. Its registered primary, verbatim, is 'Number of Participants With BARC Type 2, 3, or 5' -- described as 'first occurrence of clinically relevant bleeding episode'. It is the trial's only registered primary. A harm endpoint was averaged with three efficacy composites, which is the same defect this corpus withdrew DOAC_CANCER_VTE and APIXABAN_ACS for. (3) the three ischaemic composites are not identical either: plato counts 'Death From Vascular Causes, Myocardial Infarction (MI), and Stroke' to 12 months; Triton counts 'Cardiovascular (CV) Death, Nonfatal Myocardial Infarction (MI), or Nonfatal Stroke' to 15 months -- a different death term, nonfatal restrictions the others do not carry, and a window three months longer; ISAR-REACT 5 counts 'Composite of death, myocardial infarction or stroke' to 12 months, where 'death' is unqualified and therefore all-cause. Three death definitions across three trials. (4) the heterogeneity already said so: i-squared 91.0%, Q 33.4 on 3 degrees of freedom, and the per-trial estimates run from 0.55 to 1.37 -- ISAR-REACT 5 points the opposite way to the rest. Withdrawn: the displayed value 0.8371 (0.6576-1.0657) is not an estimate of anything one question could ask.

Arm labels are inverted in this objectThe registry's posted results give ticagrelor 864/9333 and clopidogrel 1014/9291. This object stores 864/9333 under the label 'Clopidogrel' with role 'treatment', and 1014/9291 under 'Ticagrelor' with role 'control'.
