Estimate withdrawn. k=1, and the second trial belongs to a different drug. Read from ClinicalTrials.gov 2026-08-18. NCT01857583 tests DU-176b (edoxaban) at three doses, all typed experimental, against fondaparinux as its ACTIVE_COMPARATOR -- so it is an edoxaban trial and fondaparinux appears in it only as the control. That is the olmesartan shape and it is real here for that one trial. NCT00789399 IS a genuine fondaparinux trial: its own title reads 'A Study of the Efficacy of Preventive Dosing of Fondaparinux Sodium Versus Placebo' and its other arm is typed PLACEBO_COMPARATOR, so fondaparinux is unambiguously the tested agent even though its arm is typed ACTIVE_COMPARATOR. So this topic is not comparator-shaped -- it does contain a fondaparinux trial -- but it contains exactly one, and a single trial is reported as a single trial.

What this does not establish. Not that the contributing trial is poor -- it is registered, randomised and reports its primary. Not that the drug is ineffective. Not that no poolable question exists in this area: only that this page holds one trial that answers the question its title asks.
