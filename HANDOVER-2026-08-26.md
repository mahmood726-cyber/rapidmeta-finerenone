## A scope rule that retroactively qualifies work — read this before trusting any sweep

**A client-side change cannot be confirmed or refuted by grepping the bytes that carry it.**

We have had the built-versus-served gap the right way round all day: source checked in place
of served. This is it inverted — *served source* checked in place of *rendered DOM*. On
2026-08-26 the indicator chips were reported missing from the live homepage on the strength
of a byte check. Measured in a browser on the served page: 532 cards, 532 indicator rows,
2,128 chips, zero console errors. Nothing was broken.

**The fingerprint is exact and worth memorising**: three or four phrase hits that look like
sparse, page-level rendering are the string literals *inside the script* that will populate
every card. `not measured 2`, `not assessed 3`, `readiness 6` across a 532-card index is not
a partial render; it is a script that has not run yet, counted as though it were output.

**What this qualifies.** Every deployed-page check either lane ran this evening is valid for
server-rendered content and **silently invalid for anything injected at runtime**. The old
integrity badge, the e156 claim chips and the new indicators are all runtime-injected. Any
sweep over those needs a browser. Any past sweep over them told us nothing — including the
ones that reported success.

## The NMA generic panels — tomorrow's first item

**43 pages ship all six generic pooling engines and are genuine networks.** Cumulative, TSA,
leave-one-out, funnel, Bayesian and meta-regression, pooling studies that contribute to
different contrasts as though they were replicates of one comparison.

**Quote the qualifier every time: 43 is EXPOSURE, not confirmed misrendering.** It means the
page ships the engines and has a populated network config. Whether each one actually pools
across contrasts is a render property and needs a browser. I have confirmed exactly one that
way — `HFREF_NMA_AUTO_FULL_REVIEW.html`, live: Bayesian `OR 0.81 [0.74–0.88] · τ 0.15 ·
k=28`, Cumulative `27 pools [umbrella]`, TSA `conclusive (boundary crossed), ≥100% of RIS`.

**The harm is false precision, not meaninglessness.** `[0.74–0.88]` is *tighter* than any
real network estimate, and TSA declaring the evidence conclusive tells a reader the question
is settled. **A meaningless number invites scepticism; a confidently narrow one invites
belief.** That is why suppression is right and a caveat is not: there is nothing to qualify.

**The distribution matters more than the 43.** 24 of the 43 are not named NMA
(`OBESITY_DRUGS_REVIEW`, `PAH_THERAPY_REVIEW`, `ICU_SEDATION_REVIEW`, `MIGRAINE_ACUTE_REVIEW`
and 20 more). 81 of the 100 NMA-named pages are not networks at all — machinery, no config.
**A filename sweep would have found 19, missed 24, and confidently listed 81 pages that do
not have the problem** — wrong in both directions at once, which is a shape we had not hit
before today.

**Two wrong answers preceded the right one, and both looked like answers.** Keying on
`t1`/`t2` contrast rows gave **1**; keying on `NMA_CONFIG|NMAEngine|nma-league` gave **744**,
matching shared template CSS on pages with no network. There are three config shapes: inline,
by-reference, and contrast rows. **Both were caught by the implausibility of the number, not
by inspection** — 99 NMA-named pages with zero networks is not a corpus, and neither is 744
of 745. **A single control in either direction would have passed both broken versions.** That
is the argument for two-each-way, settled.

`HFREF_NMA_AUTO_FULL_REVIEW.html` itself: documented, deliberately unfixed, in the prune's
drop set. Its protocol link 404s, its badge says v1.0 while the link says v1.1, and
`protocols/INDEX.md` lists 83 of 1,193 protocols and contains zero occurrences of "hfref".

**Left open rather than guessed: the audit read eligibility criteria from a protocol that is
not in this repository.** Where did it read them? Either a copy exists somewhere we do not
know about, or the criteria were inferred. Both are worth knowing, and the answer changes how
much weight that half of the finding carries.

### Two rules this lane is leaving behind

**The direction of a false positive decides how much validation it needs.** A manufactured
category about our own pages costs a retraction. A manufactured outcome-switching claim about
a named trial is an accusation. Same mechanism, same silence, very different cost — so any
instrument whose false positives land on a third party needs a reading step before
publication, not merely a control. This is not hypothetical: the naive D5 diff would have
published a typo correction, "microbiological" → "microbiologically", as evidence of outcome
switching in a named trial.

**A failed probe is not a negative result.** Writing one down as one is how "we could not
look" becomes "there is nothing there" three reports later. The PMC open-access endpoint
404ed on all three tigecycline records, so whether supplementary protocol files exist is
**unknown**, not absent.

## Two items added at close, 2026-08-27

### Every claim about a document is a claim about a version

A sibling lane pulled SOLOIST `NCT03521934` version 0 (30 April 2018, 46 revisions): primary
"Cardiovascular (CV) death or hospitalization for heart failure", secondary "Total number of
heart failure events". Today those two have swapped and the registry lists **zero
time-to-first outcomes at any rank**. With SCORED version 0, **both pivotal sotagliflozin
trials registered a first-event primary and both changed to a total-occurrence primary during
conduct** — sayable from the record rather than from inference.

It also corrected our own prose. The store said *"the registry posts this trial's composite
only as total occurrences"* — **true of the current record, false of version 0.** We were not
wrong about the registry; **we described a versioned document without naming the version.**

⇒ **Any claim about a registry, a protocol, a page or a store is a claim about a version.
Name it.** This is the build-stamp finding — an instruction, not a label — arriving in our
prose about someone else's document. The registration-history endpoint
(`/api/int/studies/<NCT>/history`, and `/history/<n>` for a version; the documented
`/api/v2/.../history` 404s) is what makes the version nameable.

### The corpus cannot check its own arithmetic

`scripts/lane_rob/gate_estimate_reconciles.py` recomputes every stored per-trial effect from
the counts stored beside it.

| | records | share |
