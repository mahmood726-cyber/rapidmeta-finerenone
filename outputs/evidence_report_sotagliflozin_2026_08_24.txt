EVIDENCE REPORT

Sotagliflozin compared with placebo in two phase 3 trials. Three questions, each
answered on its own and never combined with the others:
   1. all occurrences of cardiovascular death, heart failure hospitalisation or
      urgent heart failure visit, counting repeat events;
   2. the time to the FIRST such event;
   3. the time to the first cardiovascular death, non-fatal myocardial infarction
      or non-fatal stroke.

WHO WAS STUDIED
   adults with type 2 diabetes recently hospitalised for worsening heart failure, with the first dose given before discharge in about half and a median of two days after it in the rest
   adults with type 2 diabetes, chronic kidney disease with an estimated glomerular filtration rate of 25 to 60, and cardiovascular risk; heart failure was NOT an entry requirement

WHICH TRIALS
   NCT03521934   SOLOIST-WHF    1222 randomised; phase 3, double-blind, randomised one to one against placebo, STOPPED EARLY because the sponsor withdrew funding; 2021
   NCT03315143   SCORED         10584 randomised; phase 3, double-blind, randomised one to one against placebo, STOPPED EARLY because the sponsor withdrew funding, and with its PRIMARY ENDPOINT CHANGED during the trial; 2021
   Each trial's registry entry was read in full on 2026-08-18, including its primary,
   secondary and other registered outcomes, not only the outcomes used here.

HOW FAR THESE RESULTS CAN BE TRUSTED
Risk of bias was assessed with RoB 2 separately for each reported result, rather
than once for each trial, because the same trial can be at different risk of bias
for different endpoints.

   randomisation                      no information in 4
   deviations                         no information in 4
   missing outcome data               no information in 4
   measurement                        no information in 3, some concerns in 1
   selection of reported result       high in 3, low in 1

   15 of the 20 domain judgements are NO INFORMATION. That is not a finding
   that the trials were well or badly run: it records that neither trial's
   protocol or statistical analysis plan was obtained, so those domains
   could not be judged at all.

The hazard ratios above are the ones the trials themselves published. Where a
standard error was needed to combine them, it was calculated back from the
published confidence interval; no estimate here was recomputed from raw data.

WHAT THIS REPORT DOES NOT CONTAIN
It reports relative effects only. It holds no arm-level event counts and no
control-arm event rate, and therefore no absolute risk reduction and no number
needed to treat. A hazard ratio of 0.72 cannot be turned into a benefit for a
particular patient without those figures, and they are not here.
It contains no safety or adverse-event data of any kind. Nothing here should be read
as showing that the benefits outweigh the harms, because the harms were not
collected for this report.
It does not record how long the trials followed participants.
No bibliographic database search was run. The trials were identified from named
registrations, so an eligible trial that nobody named would not appear here, and
this is not a systematic review.
No protocol was registered in advance for this report.
It reports what these two trials measured. It does not interpret those measurements,
recommend practice, or place them beside the other SGLT2 inhibitor trials.
