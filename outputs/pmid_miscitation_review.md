Generated by `scripts/adjudicate_pmid_conflicts.py` + `scripts/map_miscitation_apps.py`.
Method: detect NCTs carrying different PMIDs across apps → resolve every PMID's
title/year via PubMed → flag records that show a trial's PRIMARY outcome but cite
a DESIGN (rationale/protocol) or SUB (post-hoc/secondary/QoL/review) paper.

## Deferred — NO clean primary among the cited PMIDs (manual review needed)

These showed a PMID conflict where BOTH cited papers are non-primary, so there
was no verified target to substitute. Do NOT auto-fix; needs the true
primary-results PMID looked up and added.

- **NCT03334396 (BREEZE-AD baricitinib)** — `BARICITINIB_AD_AUTO_FULL` cites
  33222559 (PRO sub-paper); sibling `ATOPIC_DERM_NMA` cites 34437922 which is a
  *review* ("JAK inhibitors in the treatment of atopic dermatitis"), not a trial
  primary. True BREEZE-AD1/AD2 primary not present in either.
- **NCT03619213 (DELIVER dapagliflozin)** — both cited PMIDs are DELIVER
  sub-analyses (38265835 mode-of-death post-hoc; 36029465 frailty prespecified).
  DELIVER primary (Solomon, NEJM 2022) not cited by these apps.
- **NCT01969838 (SIMPLIFY-1 momelotinib)** — 37042865 is a long-term integrated
  analysis; primary-results PMID needs confirming.

## Not investigated

The remaining ~47 cross-app PMID conflicts (of 66 total) had no DESIGN/SUB tag on
any member — i.e. both are plausibly primary/results papers (a trial can have a
main paper plus a co-primary or pivotal sibling). These were not adjudicated and
may be legitimate. `outputs/pmid_conflicts.json` lists all 66.
