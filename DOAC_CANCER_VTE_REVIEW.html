Estimate withdrawn. THE THREE POOLED TRIALS DO NOT SHARE ONE ENDPOINT, AND ONE WHOLE COMPONENT IS PRESENT IN ONE AND ABSENT FROM THE OTHER TWO. Hokusai VTE-Cancer's registered primary, verbatim, is 'Number of Participants With Adjudicated Recurrent Venous Thromboembolism (VTE) OR MAJOR BLEEDING EVENT' at 12 months. CARAVAGGIO's is 'Recurrent Venous Thromboembolism' at 6 months, which its registry description defines as proximal DVT of the lower limbs, DVT of the upper limb, and PE -- NO BLEEDING COMPONENT. The row carrying SELECT-D's data is a recurrent-VTE effect at 6 months. So one trial's endpoint counts a HARM and the other two do not. THIS IS NOT A NEUTRAL MISMATCH AND ITS DIRECTION IS KNOWN. Edoxaban caused more major bleeding than dalteparin, so including bleeding moves Hokusai from a recurrent-VTE hazard ratio of 0.71 to a composite of 0.97 -- the registry's own Cox analysis gives 0.97 (0.696 to 1.359). Pooling it with two efficacy-only endpoints mixes a harm signal into an efficacy estimate AND DRAGS THE POOL TOWARD THE NULL. SEPARATELY AND SUFFICIENT ON ITS OWN: ONE OF THE THREE TRIALS IS MISIDENTIFIED BY REGISTRATION. NCT02583191 is CONKO-011 (AIO-Studien-gGmbH, Germany, enrolment 246), whose registered primary is patient-reported treatment satisfaction measured to 4 weeks. The data on that row is SELECT-D's. See identity_conflict on the trial. SEPARATELY AGAIN: THE PUBLISHED NUMBER IS NOT RECONSTRUCTABLE FROM ANYTHING THIS PAGE OR THIS OBJECT HOLDS. The index card served 'Published: HR 0.55 (0.30-1.00), k=4'. This object's pooled value is OR 0.7290 (0.4914 to 1.0817) at k=3. The page's own benchmark block holds 0.63 and 0.71 and no 0.55. Ten candidate pools of the page's own four published hazard ratios -- DerSimonian-Laird, REML and fixed-effect, each with Wald and Hartung-Knapp intervals, plus every three-trial subset -- give 0.60, 0.58, 0.64, 0.49, 0.54 and 0.75, and NONE gives 0.55 (0.30 to 1.00). The string '0.55' occurs on the page only inside CSS declarations and a chart legend position.

WHAT IS CONFIRMED, STATED AS PROMINENTLY AS WHAT IS WRONG. (1) HOKUSAI VTE-CANCER IS EXTRACTED CORRECTLY IN EVERY RESPECT. Its stored counts 67/522 and 71/524 are the registry's own primary-outcome counts exactly, and the page's hazard ratio 0.97 (0.70 to 1.36) is the registry's Cox analysis 0.97 (0.696 to 1.359). (2) CARAVAGGIO IS EXTRACTED CORRECTLY. Its stored counts 32/576 and 46/579 are the registry's own primary-outcome counts exactly. (3) THE SELECT-D ROW'S NUMBERS ARE INTERNALLY COHERENT AND CORRECT FOR SELECT-D: 8/101 = 7.9 per cent and 18/102 = 17.6 per cent, matching the percentages in the verbatim sentence quoted beside them. The trial data is right; only the registration key is wrong. (4) ARM ROLES AND ARM LABELS ARE CORRECT ON ALL THREE TRIALS -- edoxaban against dalteparin, rivaroxaban against low-molecular-weight heparin, apixaban against dalteparin -- and each label matches its registration's own arm titles. No arm is inverted here, which is worth saying because the two topics before this one both had inverted labels. WHAT THIS WITHDRAWAL DOES NOT ESTABLISH: NOT that direct oral anticoagulants and low-molecular-weight heparin cannot be compared in cancer-associated VTE, and NOT that these trials cannot be pooled. Three of the four register or report a recurrent-VTE endpoint and those are comparable. What cannot stand is THIS pool: one endpoint that counts bleeding averaged with two that do not, a misidentified registration, a fourth trial dropped in silence, and a headline reconstructable from nothing. THE REPLACEMENT QUESTION IS IDENTIFIED AND DELIBERATELY NOT TAKEN: pool RECURRENT VTE ONLY across all four, using Hokusai's recurrent-VTE hazard ratio rather than its composite. It is not taken because computing the ten candidate pools above meant computing that one too, so its answer is already known to whoever would choose the question -- the same rule that parked ABLATION_AF, SGLT2_CKD and DOAC_AF. It also cannot be done honestly until the NCT02583191 identity is resolved, because one of the four trials would be entering it under another trial's registration. PARKED.
