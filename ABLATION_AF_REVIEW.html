Results

Number of Participants With Composite of Total Mortality, Disabling Stroke, Serious Bleeding, or Cardiac Arrest in Patie. These 4 trials are not pooled, and the reason is stated rather than the outcome being quietly omitted: The four trials measure four different things, and the registry states all four.
