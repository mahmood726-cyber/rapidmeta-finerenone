RapidMeta · Living Evidence Synthesis

It began with one molecule.

The question was narrow — does protect the failing kidney? — and the answer was built not by mining PDFs, but by reading the trial registry itself, then rebuilt each time the evidence moved. That one living review became a method. The portfolio now runs to 947 living reviews — of which 617 are provenance-verified (only the curated 17 benchmark apps are externally validated) — each pools registered trials traced to their publications (PMID + registry), and the curated core is benchmarked against published meta-analyses and R metafor. The remainder are live but provenance-pending, shown in the dashboard without a pooled estimate. Open any one, and follow the evidence to its source.

What has and has not been checked on these reviews. Three things are tracked separately below, because passing one of them says nothing about the others. Extraction identifier checks — run …, covering … of … cards, with … flagged and … not measured at all. These checks read trial identifiers — PubMed and registry numbers, drug names, whether a cited value can be found. They do not examine pooling, heterogeneity, risk of bias, certainty, or whether the outcome analysed is the one the trial registered. Scientific validity — not assessed on any review here. No such assessment exists in this corpus, and none is inferred from the identifier checks above. Submission readiness — each page computes its own, and … of … currently state they are ready. Reviews here are read by AI. Where two readers assessed risk of bias, both readings are shown on the page and no adjudication between them has been recorded. Nothing here has been verified by a human. full table → portfolio pools → methodology

From registries, not PDFs

Trials, arms and outcomes are read from structured registry data — so a review is refreshed as the evidence grows, not frozen at the date of publication.

Cochrane-grade, in the browser

REML and HKSJ pooling, prediction intervals, network meta-analysis, GRADE — the full Cochrane v6.5 apparatus, with nothing to install.

Auditable to the trial

Every estimate is cross-checked against R metafor and the published record — and every number traces back to the registration it came from.
