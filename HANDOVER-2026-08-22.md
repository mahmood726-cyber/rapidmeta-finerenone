**To resume the corpus rebuild:** `python scripts/../<scratch>/rollout_default_tab.py`. It takes
a lock at `outputs/.rollout_default_tab.lock`, skips anything already in its ledger, and sets
`RM_ALLOW_MANUSCRIPT_SHRINK=1` **inside the script** — an exported shell variable does not reach
the child process, which cost three aborted starts to establish.

**Why the shrink allowance is needed and is not a bypass.** This rebuild deliberately removes
text: the verbatim second-assessor dump and the sentences in which the paper narrated its own
construction. Every page loses characters on purpose, which is the case the guard names as
"genuinely intended". It is not covering a regression.

## Open findings, recorded not acted on

Asked cold, without our conclusion, it found:

> The Abstract states `hazard ratio 0.7636 (0.7062 to 0.8258) across 3 trials` and the
> Introduction says the review "pools 3 randomised trials". The object also holds
> `cvdeath_or_whf_first` at **k=4, withdrawn**. A reader is not told that a four-trial analysis

and rendered on its own outcome; it is not visible from the Abstract, which is where a reader
