# The 26 infectious-disease candidates, read on all four limbs

The outcome-limb screen passed 26. Reading intervention, comparator, participants **and**
outcome on every seeded registration separates them into three groups — and the middle
group is the one that mattered most to get right.

---

## The split

| | n |
|---|---|
| **survive to a build** | **13** |
| close on a genuine limb failure | 11 |
| seed k=0 — not builds at all | 4 |

**13 of 62 ID topics survive to a build**, against **9 of 53** in cardiology. The corpus
picture holds: **roughly one topic in five supports a pool, and four in five do not.**

---

## Label variance — the topic KEEPS, and this is the error worth avoiding

**A topic wrongly closed destroys a legitimate synthesis and publishes the destruction as
rigour.** Where the registry strings differ but the disease is plainly the same, the flag
is our reading being too literal, not a defect:

| topic | flagged on | why it keeps |
|---|---|---|
| `MALARIA_ACT` | *malaria; malaria fever; malaria,falciparum; plasmodium falciparum malaria; uncomplicated malaria* | **five strings, one disease.** All five register an adequate-clinical-and-parasitological-response primary |
| `NIRSEVIMAB_INFANT_RSV` | *RSV infections; RSV immunization; healthy volunteer* | all three register **medically-attended RSV lower-respiratory-tract infection** |
| `AGYW_HIV_PREP` | *HIV infections; HIV prevention* | all five register **HIV seroconversion or incidence** in women |
| `HIV_PREP_INJECTABLE` | *HIV infections; pre-exposure prophylaxis of HIV-1* | all three register **incident HIV infection** |

## Genuine limb failures — 11 close

| topic | limb | what the registrations say |
|---|---|---|
| `CEFTAZIDIME_AVIBACTAM` | outcome + participants | complicated **intra-abdominal** vs complicated **urinary tract** infection — and **both primaries are adverse-event counts**, not efficacy |
| `CEFEPIME_TAZ` | outcome | one primary is **treatment-emergent adverse events**, the other composite clinical success |
| `ANIDULAFUNGIN_CANDIDA` | outcome | one primary is **adverse events**, two are global response |
| `CEFTOLOZANE_INFECTION` | participants + outcome | cUTI/pyelonephritis, cIAI, and **HAP/VAP with all-cause mortality** — three infections, three endpoints |
| `CEFTOLOZANE_TAZ` | same | **the same three trials as the row above** |
| `ERTAPENEM` | participants + outcome | **urinary tract** infection with a microbiological primary vs **intra-abdominal** with a clinical one |
| `ERTAPENEM_INFECT` | same | **the same two trials as the row above** |
| `MEROPENEM` | outcome | emergence of resistance; all-cause mortality at day 28; all-cause mortality at day 14 |
| `PLAZOMICIN_INFECTION` | participants + outcome | cUTI microbiological eradication ×2 vs **bloodstream infection with all-cause mortality** |
| `IVERMECTIN_LF` | participants | two trials of **lymphatic filariasis** (microfilarial clearance) and one of **malaria** (clinical malaria episodes) |
| `FIDAXOMICIN_CDI` | **intervention** | fidaxomicin is **experimental in 0 of 3** trials and the comparator in all three — **the fourth instance of the OLMESARTAN shape** |

**The beta-lactam cluster is the dominant pattern here**, as prevention-vs-treatment was
predicted to be: seven topics pooling complicated intra-abdominal infection with
complicated urinary tract infection, pyelonephritis or hospital-acquired pneumonia. Those
are different infections at different sites with different cure definitions.

## The 13 that survive

`CAB_PREP_HIV` · `LENACAPAVIR_PREP` · `GEPOTIDACIN_URINARY_TRACT` · `LEFAMULIN_CABP` ·
`LEFAMULIN_CAP` · `MENACYW_HEALTHY_VOLUNTEERS` · `MALARIA_ACT` · `NIRSEVIMAB_INFANT_RSV` ·
`AGYW_HIV_PREP` · `HIV_PREP_INJECTABLE` · `ANIDULAFUNGIN_FUNGAL` · `CEFTAROLINE` ·
`ERAVACYCLINE_INFECTION`

**Three carry caveats to settle during the build**, recorded now rather than discovered
later:

- **`ANIDULAFUNGIN_FUNGAL`** — one trial enrols **neutropenic** patients and one does not.
  Both register global response. Kept because the outcome matches, but the population
  question is real and must be answered on the page.
- **`CEFTAROLINE`** — three trials are pneumonia with clinical-cure primaries; a fourth
  mixes **community-acquired pneumonia with complicated skin and soft-tissue infection**.
  The three cohere; the fourth needs a decision.
- **`AGYW_HIV_PREP`** — one trial's registered primary is **"person-years of follow-up"**,
  which is not an effect measure. That row needs reading before it contributes.
