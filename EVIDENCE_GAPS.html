Evidence-gap & search-audit record

Question: of the app pages that render with fewer than two poolable trials (k<2), how many are genuine evidence gaps (≤1 RCT exists in the world) versus our own registry-only search failure (RCTs exist in PubMed / Europe PMC that the registry snapshot missed)?

Method: three-layer existence search — ClinicalTrials.gov/AACT (already run) → PubMed (NCBI) → Europe PMC open-access. Run over all 260 k<2 apps (35 k=0, 225 k=1). A datum counts as available if RCTs are found in ANY layer.

Result: 260 → 259 rescued, 1 genuine evidence gap

259 of 260 apps (99.6%) have ≥2 RCTs findable in PubMed / Europe PMC. Their low k was our search failure, not an absence of evidence — they are KEPT. (Adding the found trials is the pending extraction step; the pages honestly show their current k in the meantime.)
