=== WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES ===

A PRIMARY-SOURCE, PER-GROUP, COMPARATIVE FIGURE for sexually transmitted infections, from a
trial that names them as a SECONDARY OBJECTIVE rather than reporting them incidentally.

  dapivirine  32.0 infections per 100 person-years (95% CI, 29.6 to 34.5)
  placebo     31.1 infections per 100 person-years (95% CI, 27.7 to 34.6)

The intervals overlap almost entirely and the trial's own reading is "similar in the two
groups".

=== WHAT IT IS NOT, AND THIS MATTERS MORE THAN THE NUMBERS ===

It is an INCIDENCE RATE PER 100 PERSON-YEARS for a COMPOSITE of curable sexually transmitted
infections. The comparator reports RISK RATIOS FOR INDIVIDUAL ORGANISMS -- chlamydia
RR 0.97, syphilis RR 1.70. These are different quantities on three axes at once: composite
versus per-organism, rate versus risk, and person-time versus participants. Neither confirms
nor contradicts the other, and no pooling of the two is possible or attempted.

Per-organism counts are stated to be in Table S10 of the Supplementary Appendix, which is a
separate document this excerpt does not cover.
