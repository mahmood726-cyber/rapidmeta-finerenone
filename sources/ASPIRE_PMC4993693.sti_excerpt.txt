Baeten JM et al. Use of a Vaginal Ring Containing Dapivirine for HIV-1 Prevention in Women.

WHY THIS FILE EXISTS. The page carried chlamydia and syphilis figures borrowed from the
COMPARATOR (Cochrane CD007961.pub3) because that was the only source held for them, and every
blinded judge that raised outcome scope gave the axis to the comparator. A review whose outcomes
are borrowed from its comparator cannot be decisively better than it on scope. This is the
primary-source read that was run to replace them.

=== EVERY MENTION OF SEXUALLY TRANSMITTED INFECTIONS IN THE ASPIRE PRIMARY REPORT BODY ===

[Study Procedures]
"All participants received a package of HIV-1 prevention services, including counseling with
respect to HIV-1 risk reduction, partner HIV-1 testing, treatment of sexually transmitted
infections in participants and partners, and free condoms."

[Results — Safety]
"Incident sexually transmitted infections occurred at a similar rate in the two groups ()."

[Discussion — closing paragraph]
"In the placebo group in our trial, the annualized HIV-1 incidence was more than 4%, despite
monthly HIV-1 testing and risk-reduction counseling, testing of male partners, screening and
treatment of sexually transmitted infections, and provision of free condoms."

=== WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT ===

ESTABLISHED, at trial-report tier:
  * ASPIRE reports incident STIs as a COMPOSITE and states the direction: a SIMILAR RATE in the
    two groups. This is a primary-source statement and it replaces reliance on the comparator
    for the direction of effect.

NOT ESTABLISHED — and this is the finding, not a gap to be filled from elsewhere:
  * NO PER-ORGANISM BREAKDOWN APPEARS ANYWHERE IN THE BODY TEXT. The words chlamydia,
    gonorrhoea, syphilis, trichomoniasis and herpes/HSV occur ZERO times in the full text.
  * NO COUNTS, NO DENOMINATORS, NO EFFECT ESTIMATE for STIs of any kind. The sentence carries a
    reference to a table or figure which the retrieved rendering resolves to empty parentheses,
    so the figure exists in the published article but is not in what was retrieved.

  ⛔ THEREFORE THE COMPARATOR'S PER-ORGANISM FIGURES CANNOT BE CHECKED AGAINST THE ASPIRE BODY,
  and they are NOT the same quantity as what ASPIRE reports. ASPIRE reports a composite; the
  comparator reports chlamydia and syphilis separately. Treating one as evidence about the other
  would be the estimand mismatch this review refuses elsewhere, one level down.

=== THE RING STUDY ===

Nel A et al. Safety and Efficacy of a Dapivirine Vaginal Ring for HIV Prevention in Women.
N Engl J Med 2016;375:2133-2143. PMID 27959766 · doi 10.1056/NEJMoa1602046
NO PMCID. Not deposited in PubMed Central, so no open-access full text exists to retrieve.
Its ABSTRACT reports HIV-1 seroconversion, resistance mutations and serious adverse events, and
reports NO sexually-transmitted-infection outcome of any kind.

  ⇒ STATE: NOT_RETRIEVABLE_OPEN_ACCESS. Not "no effect", not "not reported by the trial" -- the
  trial may well report it in the full article. What is true is that this review cannot read it.
  The distinction matters: one is a fact about the evidence, the other is a fact about our reach.
