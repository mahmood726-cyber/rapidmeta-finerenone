## ⚠️ The first run was invalid and is reported anyway

The first round-1 extraction of the comparator **spilled into the parent review's other
interventions** — tenofovir, cellulose sulphate, PRO 2000, SAVVY. Two of three judges then
scored the comparator down for *lack of focus on dapivirine*, an axis it lost because of my
extraction rather than its own writing. **That is a PICO mismatch in our own favour**, the
mirror of the error this project refuses elsewhere.

Bounded to the dapivirine section only — **0 other-microbicide mentions**, while the comparator
keeps every one of its own strengths (RR 0.71, moderate certainty, SAE 1.12, central
randomisation, MEDLINE search) — and re-run. **The verdict was unchanged: 3 of 3, DECISIVELY.**
Both runs are recorded; the clean one is the result.

### ⭐ This corrects my own control finding

The control run concluded that **no judge named verifiability as an axis**. In round 1 **all
three did**, at 10–20%. The difference is not that the judges changed: **in the control neither
document had an audit trail, and judges name axes on which the documents actually differ.**

⇒ **An axis appears when the content does.** The control's negative was real about that pair
and wrong as a general claim about what judges value — and I stated it too broadly at the time.

## What the comparator was told it needed

Two of three named the same two gaps, unprompted:

1. **Age-stratified efficacy** — *"the most decision-relevant finding in these trials — no
   demonstrated protection in women ≤21 — is entirely absent"*
2. **Provenance for the counts** — *"does not show where 71/1313, 97/1316, 77/1300, 56/650 come
   from, does not distinguish adjudicated from registry figures, and does not disclose that one
   primary report is paywalled"*

The comparator's single retained advantage was **formal GRADE**, which one judge described as
*"a framework B could adopt without changing any of its content"*.

## ⚠️ How to read this verdict

**Two of the sections that won it are hand-written.** The currency section and the clinical
reading have no component behind them. **This measures our ceiling with those written by hand,
not what the recipe currently reaches.** The three missing components — currency, per-outcome
extraction, subgroup — are what would make this result reproducible on the other 26 topics.

**And the adjudication was never finished.** We won 3 of 3 while still carrying PENDING
certainty against the comparator's *moderate*. The certainty axis was scored 15% by one judge
and we lost it. That gap remains.
