COVID-19 vaccines versus placebo: per-vaccine efficacy against symptomatic COVID-19, for the trials that could be sourced

For each COVID-19 vaccine whose trial data could be sourced here, what was the risk of symptomatic laboratory-confirmed COVID-19 relative to placebo in its own trial? This is a per-vaccine snapshot of the sourceable trials. It is NOT a systematic review of COVID-19 vaccine evidence, and it does not combine vaccines. Most vaccines absent from it are absent because their counts could not be sourced here rather than because they were assessed and excluded. Two removals are not that: a registration carrying counts that belong to a pooled analysis of several trials, and a trial of a different disease. Both are itemised below.

Symptomatic laboratory-confirmed COVID-19

No combined estimate

These trials test DIFFERENT vaccines on different platforms, against different circulating variants, in different periods, with different case definitions. They do not estimate a common quantity, so no pooled figure is computed. The reference systematic review reaches the same conclusion and reports efficacy per vaccine. An earlier build of this object did report a single pooled risk ratio across all three; it was removed rather than caveated, because a number that should not be computed is not fixed by a warning beside it.

Each row is a single vaccine in a single trial. Nothing is combined across vaccines. A crude risk ratio is computed from a trial's own counts where its denominators match its events; a trial whose denominators do not match its events carries no ratio. The efficacy beside it is what the reference systematic review reports for the same vaccine, shown so the two can be compared rather than either taken on trust. Where a trial analysed its outcome on person-time rather than as a proportion, or where the trial publication and the review report different figures for the same trial, the row says so. EVERY row here carries such a note: none of these three comparisons is exact, and the closeness of the numbers should not be read as agreement between identical quantities.

What was removed, and why

Of 12 citations in the source app, 9 were removed and 3 retained. The reduction is shown rather than absorbed.

  • per-arm counts not verifiable to a primary source — 7. These trials cited by the source app report no per-arm event counts and denominators that could be read from a primary source in this environment. They are excluded rather than reconstructed. This is a real limitation and a source of availability bias: the analysis is a convenience sample of what could be verified, NOT a systematic review of all evidence, and must not be read as one. Every excluded registration is staged with this object under a 'removed' subfolder, so the reduction can be audited and not merely counted. They sit apart from the contributing sources because they support no cell here. NCT04368728, NCT04470427, NCT04505722, NCT04611802, NCT04456595, NCT04672395, NCT04619628
  • units-of-analysis error — 1. QUOTED: NCT04324606 was carried as a ChAdOx1 trial with 30 of 5807 versus 101 of 5829. Those are POOLED interim counts drawn from a single publication that combines several trials, not this trial's data. The registration cited is COV001 -- the staged registry record gives that as its organisation study identifier, a phase 1 and 2 study with an actual enrolment of 1077 -- which cannot contain 5807 participants. The staged publication record for those counts names a second trial, COV002, alongside it. Pooling a pooled estimate beside individual trials misstates the unit of analysis, so this registration is removed rather than corrected. NCT04324606
  • wrong disease area — 1. A trial of a video game encouraging human papillomavirus vaccination in preteens, cited by a review of COVID-19 vaccine efficacy. It is not a COVID-19 trial and tests no vaccine. It had been filed under unverifiable counts, which was true but beside the point; the real defect only surfaced when the registration was staged and opened. The same pattern -- unrelated trials carried into a review by template rather than by selection -- is documented in the pneumococcal app in this corpus. NCT04627298
