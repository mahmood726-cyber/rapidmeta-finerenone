Method (stated on screen)

DerSimonian–Laird random-effects meta-analysis on the log risk ratio (inverse-variance). Hartung-Knapp-Sidik-Jonkman was deliberately NOT used: at k = 2–3 the HKSJ t-interval (1–2 degrees of freedom) is pathologically wide and would manufacture false precision in the opposite direction. With k = 3 the confidence interval is genuinely wide and should be read as uncertain, not as a precise estimate.

Honest limitations — read before citing

  • Outcome heterogeneity: BRIEF-TB's primary endpoint is a composite (TB or death from TB/unknown cause); the other two report confirmed active TB. The pure-TB count for BRIEF-TB is in its open-access table (PMC6563914) and would refine this slightly.
  • Population heterogeneity: BRIEF-TB and the PREVENT-TB substudy are HIV-positive; P4v9 is mostly HIV-negative. Random-effects accounts for this only partially (I²=0% here reflects the few, concordant trials).
  • Small k: three trials. This is a demonstration that a defensible synthesis is reachable from open sources — not a replacement for a full systematic review.
  • Source tier: counts are abstract-level. Full-text OA tables (JATS) would upgrade the provenance tier for each cell.
