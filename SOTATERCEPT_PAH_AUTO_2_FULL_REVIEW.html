Estimate withdrawn. Same drug, same placebo, same disease -- and two different kinds of endpoint. Both trials test sotatercept against placebo in pulmonary arterial hypertension. NCT03496207 registers 'Change From Baseline in Pulmonary Vascular Resistance (PVR) at 24 Weeks' -- a continuous haemodynamic measurement. NCT04896008 registers 'Time to First Confirmed Morbidity or Mortality Event' -- a time-to-event clinical composite. A change in vascular resistance and a time to a clinical event are not two estimates of one quantity, and no conversion between them is supported by either registration.

What this does not establish. Not that any of these trials is poor -- each is registered, randomised where it says so, and reports its primary. Not that the drug is ineffective. Not that no poolable question exists in this area: only that the question this page's title asks cannot be answered by pooling the trials this page carries.
