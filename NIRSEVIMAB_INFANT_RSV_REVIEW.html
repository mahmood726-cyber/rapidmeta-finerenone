The published point is 0.2605 and the house-rule point is 0.2606. THE NUMBER A READER REMEMBERS DOES NOT MOVE; what differs is the precision claim. That is why nothing downstream catches this -- every memorable quantity survives.

A FLOOR OF 1 IS APPLIED TO THE VARIANCE-INFLATION FACTOR so the adjustment can never NARROW an interval below the unadjusted one. Results

The pooled risk ratio for medically attended RSV lower respiratory tract infection was 0.261 (0.177 to 0.385) across 2 trials, against placebo.1

The trials' results were closely consistent with one another: I-squared, the share of the variation between them that is more than chance alone would produce, was 0%; the estimated variance of the true effects between trials (tau-squared) was 0, the heterogeneity test statistic Q was 0.0876, on degrees of freedom 1.2

What this does not establish: NOT that either trial is free of bias -- NO RISK-OF-BIAS ASSESSMENT IS RECORDED. NOT anything about the excluded open-label trial, which reports a large effect on its own terms and is excluded on COMPARATOR TYPE, not on quality. NOT RSV hospitalisation or mortality, neither of which is the pooled endpoint. NOT reliability at k=2 -- see Hartung-Knapp.3
