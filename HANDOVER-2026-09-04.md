## The structural finding

**The suite has no vocabulary for "known, pre-existing, not ours, already live on `main`".**
A finding in that category can only be fixed or suppressed, and suppressing it is
indistinguishable from suppressing a defect we introduced. Every ratchet here assumes the
tree it guards is the tree its baseline was written against; a branch carrying 19 commits
from other lanes breaks that assumption silently. The fix is a category — a verdict like
`PRE-EXISTING_ON_TRUNK` requiring blob evidence against `origin/main` — not an exemption.

## VICTORIA: the reviewer was right about the flip and wrong about the baseline

Measured. The control reproduced all six served nodes to **8.9e-06**.

> **A served significance verdict turning on one degree of freedom, in a small-sample

## The rule excluding VICTORIA is not in this repository

It is `ef: le40` plus `pop: chronic`, defined in `F:/E156/hfref_eightcell_fit.R` and sourced
from a path that is **not present here**. The page's own eligibility table **states no EF

`VICTORIA` occurs **once in 938,949 bytes** of that page, inside a note correcting a wrong

> **An eligibility criterion defined outside the repository can be neither audited nor

That is the general form, and it is the same shape as tonight's other findings from a new
direction: a claim whose evidence lives somewhere the checks cannot reach behaves exactly

## The poolability gate is not the lever — RETRACTS the framing of the k gap

**21 of 25 objects pool every trial they hold. The median number of trials held is two.**

The constraint is not that pooling rejects what we have. **The objects hold almost nothing.**
Correcting every refusal on the indexed set moves `k` by **one**.

So "our k is lower in 22 of 27" is not a poolability problem and not a research problem in
the way it was framed. It is an acquisition problem.

## THE FINDING THAT MATTERS MOST, and it is about the store

> **A recursive key walk across all 161 objects finds ZERO structured ejection-fraction
> fields. The 2026 ESC guideline changed the definition of HFrEF eight days ago, and there
> is no query that answers "which of our reviews does this affect". The corpus cannot be
> asked what a guideline change touches.**

For a project whose claim is **living** reviews, that is the gap that matters most, and it
is a **schema gap, not a data gap**. The EF thresholds exist — in prose, in eligibility
notes, in a fit script on a path that is not in this repository (see VICTORIA above). None
of it is queryable.

It is also the general form of the VICTORIA finding stated structurally: **a criterion that
is not a field cannot be audited, cannot be updated when the field moves, and cannot answer
a question asked of the corpus.**
