## The two-surface disagreement, accepted consciously

The served page still says this provenance is unverified; the object now says it is
resolved. Held deliberately, for one reason: **it errs toward understatement.** A page
saying "we have not verified this" when we have is a smaller wrong than the page it
replaced, which told a reader they could check numbers they could not. It should be
cleared by a proper rebuild once the 24-interval question is answered.

## What blocks the push

Everything that correct work can satisfy is satisfied: `OVERALL: PASS` on the
executable-rule suite, all 16 gates that ran PASS, every ratchet at or below baseline.

One check refuses, and it is **pre-existing on `origin/main`**:

    FAILED  scripts/lint_interactive_layer_2026_08_26.py   exit=1
        MALARIA_VACCINES_REVIEW.html   dup=6 sharedgrp=1 multichecked=1 deadfrag=0

`ssot/malaria-vaccines` **needs an owner more than this push needs to land.** Four distinct
defects on that one page today: it refuses to build (`analysis_status` absent on two
subgroups), it would lose 14 manuscript headings on rebuild, it holds 50 variantless

The hook offers two remedies — *"Fix it, or move it out of `gates/WIRED_REPO_CHECKS.json`
with a reason."* Both were declined: the first repairs another lane's page, the second
unwires a working check to get a push through, which is the act `gate8` exists to punish.

## The structural finding

**The suite has no vocabulary for "known, pre-existing, not ours, already live on `main`".**
A finding in that category can only be fixed or suppressed, and suppressing it is
indistinguishable from suppressing a defect we introduced. Every ratchet here assumes the
tree it guards is the tree its baseline was written against; a branch carrying 19 commits
from other lanes breaks that assumption silently. The fix is a category — a verdict like
`PRE-EXISTING_ON_TRUNK` requiring blob evidence against `origin/main` — not an exemption.
