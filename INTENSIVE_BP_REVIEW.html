Pooled result

Estimate withdrawn. SIX TRIALS, SIX DIFFERENT REGISTERED COMPOSITES. This is the widest endpoint divergence found in this programme, and every difference is stated in the trials' own registry records: SPRINT counts myocardial infarction, ACUTE CORONARY SYNDROME, stroke, HEART FAILURE and cardiovascular death -- five. ACCORD's blood-pressure trial counts NONFATAL myocardial infarction, NONFATAL stroke and cardiovascular death -- three, with no heart failure and no acute coronary syndrome. BPROAD counts four. ESPRIT counts five INCLUDING CORONARY OR NON-CORONARY REVASCULARISATION. STEP counts SIX, and one of them is ATRIAL FIBRILLATION, which appears in no other trial on this page. AND SPS3 IS NOT A COMPOSITE TRIAL AT ALL. Its registration declares TWO separate primary outcomes, each a single event type -- 'clinically defined ischemic stroke' and 'hemorrhagic stroke'. It counts stroke recurrence and nothing else. A stroke-only endpoint cannot be averaged into a five- or six-component cardiovascular composite. SEPARATELY: THE PAGE CANNOT AGREE WITH ITSELF ABOUT HOW MANY TRIALS THIS IS. Its include list names SIX registrations. Its embedded data carries ONE -- SPRINT. The index card published k=5. Three different counts, and no surface reconciles them. SEPARATELY AGAIN: THE PUBLISHED NUMBER CANNOT COME FROM THIS PAGE. HR 0.79 (0.71 to 0.87) is a five-trial pool, and the page holds an effect for exactly one trial. It also matches neither published benchmark this page carries.

WHAT IS CONFIRMED, STATED AS PROMINENTLY AS WHAT IS WRONG. (1) THE TRIALS ARE THE RIGHT TRIALS FOR THE QUESTION and every identity is correct: SPRINT, ACCORD, STEP, ESPRIT, BPROAD and SPS3 are the landmark intensive-versus-standard blood-pressure-target trials. Nothing irrelevant is on this list. (2) SPRINT'S OWN ROW IS SOUND: 243 of 4,678 against 319 of 4,683, HR 0.75 (0.64 to 0.89), over its registered five-component primary. (3) THE PUBLISHED COMPARISON HERE IS GENUINELY INDEPENDENT, which is worth saying because two other topics this session compared an estimate against itself. This page's benchmark database holds a Lancet 2025 individual-participant-data meta-analysis, HR 0.76 (0.71 to 0.82) over six trials and 80,220 participants, and Bundy 2017 in JAMA Cardiology, RR 0.84 (0.75 to 0.94) over 42 trials and 144,220 participants. NEITHER shares a registration with an included trial. THE DENOMINATOR HERE IS TWO, AND IT IS REAL. (4) AND THE EXTERNAL EVIDENCE SUPPORTS THE UNDERLYING CLAIM. Both independent syntheses find intensive targets better, and the withdrawn 0.79 sits between them. THIS WITHDRAWAL IS ABOUT WHAT THIS PAGE CAN SHOW, NOT ABOUT WHETHER INTENSIVE BLOOD-PRESSURE LOWERING WORKS. A reader wanting the answer should read the Lancet IPD analysis, which is cited on this page and pools the actual participant data. THE REPLACEMENT QUESTION IS IDENTIFIED AND DELIBERATELY NOT TAKEN. The defensible synthesis is over the trials whose composites genuinely share components -- and choosing which, when one counts atrial fibrillation and another counts stroke alone, is an estimand decision. It also duplicates work already done properly at individual-participant level by others. PARKED.
