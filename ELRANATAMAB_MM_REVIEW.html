Statistical methods

Inverse-variance random-effects pooling on the log-OR scale (Woolf estimator from AACT event counts), REML τ² with Hartung-Knapp-Sidik-Jonkman (HKSJ) variance correction and tk-1 critical value (Cochrane Handbook v6.5 conventions). Single-trial entries report Wald 95% CI on the log scale.

Search strategy
