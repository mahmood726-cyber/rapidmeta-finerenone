Estimate withdrawn. Evolocumab is the intervention in only one of three trials. Read from ClinicalTrials.gov 2026-08-18. NCT01652703 does test evolocumab against placebo in Japanese patients with hypercholesterolaemia. But NCT03060577 is ORION-3, an inclisiran extension trial whose arms are 'Inclisiran-only' (experimental) and 'Switching' (ACTIVE_COMPARATOR, inclisiran after evolocumab) -- an inclisiran trial in which evolocumab appears only inside a switching arm. And NCT04992065 tests NNC0385-0434, an oral PCSK9 inhibitor, at three doses against placebo, with subcutaneous evolocumab as its ACTIVE_COMPARATOR. So two of the three belong to other drugs, and this is the olmesartan shape -- the third instance of it in cardiology, after OLMESARTAN_HTN itself and ENOXAPARIN_VTE. For the question this title asks, k=1.
