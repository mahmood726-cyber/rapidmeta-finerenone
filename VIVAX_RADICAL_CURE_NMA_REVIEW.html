P. vivax radical cure — recurrence by 180 days

Arm-based binary network meta-analysis · 5 trials · N = 2153 randomised / 2071 evaluable · 7 nodes · generated 2026-07-30 · staged, not published

The estimand is RECURRENCE, not relapse

Unlike falciparum, PCR genotyping does not resolve vivax relapse: hypnozoites are frequently genetically heterologous to the primary infection, so relapse, reinfection and recrudescence cannot be separated. EFFORT's post-hoc genotyping found the majority of recurrences were heterologous — homologous in only 9/24 (37.5%), 11/23 (47.8%) and 12/31 (38.7%) of recurrences by arm. The outcome is therefore named recurrence by 180 days throughout, and the ambiguity is an irreducible limit of the question, not a limitation of this analysis.

Which N?

2153 patients were randomised across the five trials; the analysed denominator is 2071 evaluable. Axis P8a conditions on evaluability, so 82 patients (3.81%) are conditioned away — all of them in the three trials that report censoring (DETECTIVE_PART1 329→304, DETECTIVE_PART2 522→482, GATHER 251→234). Axis P8b and P8c put them back under different assumptions and move the focal estimate by less than 0.01 on the odds-ratio scale.

Claim A adds nodes but adds no information to Claim B

On all three contrasts the two networks share, the estimates are identical to three decimal places (ratio 1.000). That is not a coincidence and not a bug: it is the arithmetic signature of the fragility below. TQ 50/100/600 and PQ 7 d are each fitted by their own saturated within-trial contrasts, so they contribute zero to Q and zero information to the core contrasts. R netmeta's netsplit agrees independently: the direct-evidence proportion is 1.00 for the key PQ 3.5-vs-TQ 300 edge, so indirect evidence contributes essentially nothing anywhere in this network.

What explains the spread — and what does not

It is the partner blood-stage drug, and the multiverse is where that shows. Splitting on axis P3 gives OR 1.070 (0.753–1.521), I² 37.2% across the three chloroquine-partner trials versus OR 3.841 in the single DHA-piperaquine trial. INSPECTOR's own published adjusted estimate agrees (odds of relapsing 4.57, 1.75–11.97), and EFFORT replicates the direction independently: 22.4% recurrence in its tafenoquine group in Indonesia, the site where the partner was DHA-piperaquine.

The Q decomposition does not support that attribution, and an earlier version of this page wrongly said it did. Two reasons, both checkable above:

  • “Design” in decomp.design() means node-set geometry, not partner drug. INSPECTOR (DHA-piperaquine) shares its design {no-therapy, PQ 3.5, TQ 300} with DETECTIVE Part 2 (chloroquine), so the partner-drug contrast sits inside the within-design term — Q = 5.00 (df 2, p = 0.082), which is not significant. The significant between-design term (Q = 12.05, df 4, p = 0.017) is driven by DETECTIVE Part 1, the unique six-arm design — and Part 1 is a chloroquine trial.
  • The split is not invariant to the node set. On the robust core the same total Q (17.041) divides the other way round: within-design 13.56 (df 5, p = 0.019), between-design 3.48 (df 1, p = 0.062). A quantity that reverses when you drop four single-trial nodes cannot carry a causal claim about partner drug.

Edge-specific τ² — the common-τ² CI is too narrow

The network model assumes one τ² across all contrasts. With 14 of 17 edges single-trial, that assumption borrows strength this edge has not earned. Estimating τ² for this edge alone (Paule-Mandel, k = 5, τ² = 0.5584) gives OR 1.067 (0.516–2.210) against the network's 1.072 (0.629–1.825). The point estimate barely moves; the interval widens by about half. Read the wider one.

Pre-registered adversary targets

Declared at Stage 0, before fitting, for the cross-family gate.

1. Recurrence ≠ relapse, irreducibly. PCR cannot separate relapse from reinfection in vivax. EFFORT's genotyping found most recurrences heterologous. The estimand stays "recurrence" everywhere in this app, including the badge.
2. The partner blood-stage drug confounds the anti-hypnozoite estimate. Piperaquine's prophylactic tail suppresses early recurrence well past day 42. INSPECTOR found TQ+DHA-PQ far worse than PQ+DHA-PQ (OR of relapsing 4.57, 95% CI 1.75–11.97); EFFORT independently replicated it, with recurrence 22.4% in its tafenoquine group in Indonesia where the partner was DHA-PQ. Axis P3 exists for this.
3. Chloroquine alone is not a placebo, and the reference node is not one thing. The "no hypnozoiticidal therapy" node is chloroquine alone in DETECTIVE Parts 1 and 2 but DHA-piperaquine alone in INSPECTOR — drugs with very different post-treatment prophylactic tails. Where chloroquine-resistant vivax circulates a "recurrence" may be blood-stage failure, not relapse.
4. CYP2D6 breaks transitivity between the PQ and TQ nodes specifically. Primaquine needs CYP2D6 activation; tafenoquine does not. INSPECTOR measured it: 67/150 (45%) were poor or intermediate metabolisers, with no significant effect detected — in a trial of 150, which is not evidence of absence.
5. The approved 300 mg tafenoquine dose is itself contested. Watson et al. (eLife 2022) vs Sharma et al. (eLife 2024) dispute 300 mg versus 450 mg. EFFORT's median tafenoquine dose was 5.4 mg/kg. The TQ dose ladder in this network (50/100/300/600 mg) rests entirely on DETECTIVE Part 1.

How to check any number in this app

  • DETECTIVE Part 2 — the only three-way concordant trial. Registry NCT01376167 primary outcome "Number of Participants With Recurrence-free Efficacy at 6 Months" gives 35 / 133, 155 / 260, 83 / 129. FDA NDA 210795 Table 30 gives the same numerators and denominators. The publication (PMID 30650322) gives 522 in a 2:1:1 allocation.
  • DETECTIVE Part 1 — two-source only. NCT01376167's results section posts Part 2 alone; Part 1 has no registry results table. Publication (PMID 24360369) and FDA Table 22 agree to the decimal on all six arms and all six confidence intervals.
  • GATHER — three different odds ratios are all legitimately GATHER's own, and the forest plot above shows 1.125, not the 1.141 quoted in the FDA review. They are not in conflict; they are different estimators of the same contrast:
    • 1.125 — crude, observed recurrence (42/154 vs 20/80). This is what the fit uses, because axis P8a conditions on evaluability across every trial in the network.
    • 1.157 — crude, missing = failure (54/166 vs 25/85), i.e. axis P8b.
    • 1.141 (0.643, 2.027) — FDA Table 41's model-based logistic-regression estimate under missing = failure. It does not equal the crude 1.157 from the same counts because it is a fitted model, not a cross-product; no count pair yields 1.141.
    None of these is the abstract's 1.81, which comes from a pre-planned patient-level meta-analysis pooling GATHER with DETECTIVE Part 2. The arithmetic proves it — 426 = 260 + 166 and 214 = 129 + 85. Using it would double-count DETECTIVE Part 2.
  • INSPECTOR — the registry and the publication were never in conflict. CT.gov posts the crude proportions (6/50, 11/50, 26/50 = 12%, 22%, 52%); the abstract quotes the Kaplan-Meier estimates (11.2%, 21.0%, 52.0%). Different estimators, both correct, both shown.
  • EFFORT — arm-level first-recurrence counts 34/295, 35/305, 49/301 come from the full text (PMID 41690325). Its confidence intervals are 97.55%, not 95%, because alpha was spent at an interim look; they are converted on the log-hazard-ratio scale, never treated as 95%.
  • The fit itself — nma_fit.py reproduces R netmeta's fixed-effect log-ORs and standard errors to a maximum absolute difference of 4.83e-08, inside the 1e-06 tolerance. Q, df, p and I² reproduce exactly, as do all five per-trial ORs on the focal edge.

Limits carried forward, stated rather than smoothed over

  • Mixed estimators coexist. Crude proportions, Kaplan-Meier cumulative incidence and hazard ratios all appear across these five trials; KM exceeds crude wherever censoring is non-trivial. Axis P8 makes the choice explicit — and the answer barely moves (1.072 / 1.063 / 1.073).
  • EFFORT is open-label and reports 97.55% CIs; the other four are double-blind with 95% CIs. It is also the only trial deliberately measuring effectiveness (unsupervised dosing) rather than efficacy — axis P6, where the estimate moves most (1.249 supervised vs 0.667 unsupervised).
  • The reference node is not a placebo node and is not even one intervention: chloroquine alone in DETECTIVE, DHA-piperaquine alone in INSPECTOR.
  • Proportional hazards is violated on INSPECTOR's TQ-vs-PQ contrast. Its authors call the HR unreliable and use OR 4.57 (1.75–11.97). A single pooled HR on that edge would mislead, which is why this analysis pools odds ratios throughout.
  • IMPROV (N = 2336) is excluded entirely. It reports incidence risk at day 28, day 42 and 1 year — there is no 180-day row anywhere in the paper. The person-year rate was not back-converted and the 180-day risk was not interpolated. It would have been the largest trial in the network.
  • Three proposed nodes do not exist at this estimand and are declared absent rather than improvised: PQ 14 d high dose (7 mg/kg) has no 180-day evidence at all; PQ weekly × 8 wk has no eligible trial; paediatric tafenoquine is single-arm only.
  • Only NDA 210795 (Krintafel) backs this network. NDA 210607 (Arakoda) is a prophylaxis approval whose trials randomise uninfected people; it supplies zero efficacy nodes here. Two tafenoquine FDA packages exist, but only one is evidence for radical cure.
  • Tafenoquine has no EMA authorisation or scientific opinion (verified 2026-07-30): absent from the EMA medicines register, and not among the eleven EU-M4all/Article 58 opinions EMA enumerates. Its non-FDA regulatory basis is TGA Australia via WHO prequalification (MA203 / MA204, 2024-12-04). The EU-M4all list is dated July 2020, so a later opinion would not appear in it.

Methods

Contrast-based random-effects network meta-analysis (Lu & Ades parameterisation) on the log odds ratio of recurrence. Each trial contributes contrasts against its own baseline arm with the shared-baseline covariance Var = vbase + vk, Cov = vbase; the random-effects structure adds τ² on the diagonal and τ²/2 off it. τ² is estimated by REML, not DerSimonian-Laird, which is materially downward-biased at k = 5. Where a cell has too few contrasts to identify τ², it is fixed at 0 and labelled as not estimable rather than left to a flat likelihood. Continuity correction is applied only if a trial actually has a zero cell; none does here.

Validation

Independently reproduced in R netmeta. Fixed-effect log-ORs and SEs agree to 4.83e-08 (tolerance 1e-06); Q = 17.041, df = 6, p = 0.0091 and I² reproduce exactly, as do the five per-trial ORs. The Q decomposition and netsplit figures quoted above are netmeta's own.

Provenance

Arm-level counts were taken from ClinicalTrials.gov results tables, the primary publications, and the FDA multi-discipline review for NDA 210795, and reconciled across all three where all three exist. Discrepancies are displayed, not resolved silently. The FDA review PDF ships broken font CMaps that yield confident garbage rather than an error; it was decoded per page by scoring candidate transforms against a word list, and every extracted arm then satisfied recurrence + recurrence-free + censored = n.
